Macro events, policy announcements, and institutional flow prints shape market liquidity and volatility—making preparation essential before major calendar dates.

## Concept & Scenario Prompts

1. **FII & DII Flows:** How do foreign and domestic institutional flows interact across cash and index derivatives, and why is flow context best paired with price structure rather than chased blindly?
2. **Macro & Earnings Catalysts:** Why do scheduled events—such as RBI policy decisions, inflation/growth prints, or quarterly results—often cause pre-event IV buildup, post-event volatility crush, or sharp gaps?
3. **Event Playbook Discipline:** How should position sizing, leverage, and stop placement adapt when a major announcement is minutes away?

## Quick Self-Check

* Do you check the **economic, RBI, and earnings calendar** before holding leveraged F&O positions overnight?
* Can you distinguish **sustained institutional accumulation/distribution** from a one-day headline reaction?
