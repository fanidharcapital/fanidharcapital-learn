## 1. Block Deals, Bulk Deals & Shareholding Disclosures

*   **Block Deals & Bulk Deals:**
    *   **Block Deals** are large single-transaction trades executed through a dedicated exchange block window above prescribed size thresholds, typically between institutional buyers and sellers.
    *   **Bulk Deals** are open-market trades exceeding **`0.5%` of a company's listed equity shares** in a single day, disclosed by exchanges after market hours.
    *   *Context Matters:* Look at **who bought and who sold**—a long-only domestic mutual fund or sovereign fund absorbing shares from a exiting private-equity seller reads very differently from short-term momentum flipping.
*   **Mutual Fund & Promoter Shifts:** Monthly `MF` portfolio disclosures and quarterly shareholding filings reveal whether institutional ownership is **steadily rising**, whether **promoter pledges are falling** (positive governance/balance-sheet signal), or whether promoter/insider selling is creating supply overhang.

---

## 2. Confirm With Post-Deal Price & Volume

Never buy a stock blindly just because a famous name appeared on an evening bulk-deal sheet. Watch whether **price holds above the deal price on healthy volume** over the following sessions—true institutional accumulation builds support rather than immediately breaking down.

---

## 3. Key Takeaways

*   **Block/bulk deals, `MF` holdings, and promoter stake/pledge changes** highlight where large capital is entering or exiting.
*   Always check **buyer/seller quality** and verify that **price holds above the deal zone** before trading.
