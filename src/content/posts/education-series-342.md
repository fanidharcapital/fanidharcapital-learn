Expiry day brings intense **gamma** near ATM strikes, where small spot movements cause rapid changes in option delta and premiums.

## Case A: Range-Bound Pinning Near High-OI Strike

* **Setup:** Heavy call and put open interest sits clustered around a major round index strike, with no fresh macro shock during the session.
* **Behaviour:** Spot gravitates around that strike through the afternoon as hedges balance out, causing OTM options away from the strike to decay rapidly.

## Case B: Late-Session Break & Gamma Squeeze

* **Setup:** Spot presses steadily against a crowded resistance strike late in the afternoon while call writers unwind positions.
* **Behaviour:** Once the level gives way cleanly, short-gamma hedging and option delta acceleration can drive a swift late-day push—punishing unhedged short options right at the strike.

## Key Execution Lessons

* **Respect Expiry Afternoon Leverage:** Small rupee option prices near expiry carry large percentage and gamma swings.
* **Close or Manage Short Strikes Before Settlement:** Avoid leaving near-ATM short options exposed into the close, especially in physically settled stock F&O.
