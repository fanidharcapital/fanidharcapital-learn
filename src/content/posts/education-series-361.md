Advanced options execution connects **Delta, Gamma, Theta, and Vega** directly to **implied volatility regimes, skew, and term structure**.

## Concept & Scenario Prompts

1. **Greeks Under Movement & Time:** How do **Delta** and **Gamma** shift near ATM as expiration approaches, and how do **Theta** and **Vega** pull against each other across short vs. long DTEs?
2. **Volatility Regime & Structure:** When IVR/IVP is elevated after a spike versus compressed after a quiet stretch, how do credit spreads, debit spreads, and calendar spreads differ in risk and payoff?
3. **Skew & Term Structure:** How do put skew on indices and front-month event kinks influence strike and expiration selection?

## Quick Self-Check

* Before opening an option spread, do you know **which Greek is your primary tailwind and which Greek can hurt you most** if spot or VIX moves fast?
* Do you avoid oversized short-gamma or short-vega bets right into compressed volatility or major scheduled catalysts?
