## 1. The Master Price-Action Trading Decision Checklist

Before you click "Buy" or "Sell," you must answer "Yes" to all critical questions in this checklist. If you answer "No" to any structural question, you do not have a trade.

### Phase 1: Macro Context & Regime (Daily/Weekly)
1.  **Identify the Regime:** Is the market clearly **Trending** (HH/HL or LH/LL) or **Choppy/Range-bound**? (Lesson #141)
    *   *If Range:* I am only looking for Range Reversals (fading extremes).
    *   *If Trend:* I am only looking for Trend Continuations (pullbacks/breakout-retests) in the direction of the trend.
2.  **Locate Major Structure:** Where are the nearest **Weekly/Daily Support and Resistance Zones**? (Lesson #132)
3.  **Assess Higher-Timeframe Direction:** Which way is the higher-timeframe (Anchor) trend pointing? (Lesson #143).

### Phase 2: Setup & Entry (Hourly/M15)
4.  **Structural Location:** Is my potential setup (Lesson #144) anchored precisely **at a validated structural level** (Flip Zone, Trendline, EMA, or Range Extreme)? (Lesson #140).
    *   *If Mid-Range:* **ABORT TRADE.**
5.  **Volume Confirmation:** Does the volume profile (accumulation/distribution, expansion/contraction) confirm the price action? (Lesson #142).
    *   *If Divergent:* Use extreme caution or **ABORT TRADE.**
6.  **Multi-Timeframe Confluence:** Do the Anchor, Structural, and Execution timeframes all agree on the bias? (Lesson #143).

### Phase 3: Execution & Risk Management
7.  **Wait for Trigger Candle Close:** Has the M15/H1 trigger candle fully **closed** and confirmed the footprint (Hammer, Engulfing, Inside Bar Break)? (Lesson #145).
    *   *If the candle is still forming:* **DO NOT ENTER.**
8.  **Define Stop-Loss:** Where is the **structural invalidation point** (e.g., below the Hammer wick)? (Lesson #146).
9.  **Define Profit Target:** Where is the **next higher-timeframe structural level**? (Lesson #146).
10. **Verify Reward-to-Risk:** Is the distance to the target at least **2×** the distance to the stop-loss (`1:2 R:R` minimum)? (Lesson #146).

*   **If the setup meets all 10 criteria $\rightarrow$ EXECUTE THE TRADE.**
*   **If the trade fails $\rightarrow$ JOURNAL THE TRADE** (Lesson #148) to refine your playbook criteria.
