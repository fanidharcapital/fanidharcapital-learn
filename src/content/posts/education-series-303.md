On Nifty and Bank Nifty, the first hour of cash and index derivatives trading sets the tone for institutional participation. Mapping the **Opening Range**, **Initial Balance (IB)**, and nearby **liquidity pools** keeps you from reacting blindly to the 9:15 AM IST opening bell.

## Intraday Time & Liquidity Anchors (IST)

1. **Opening Range (9:15–9:30 AM or 9:15–9:45 AM):** Captures the initial post-open repricing from overnight cues, Gift Nifty, and opening order imbalances.
2. **Initial Balance (9:15–10:15 AM):** The high-to-low range of the first trading hour, where institutional desks and liquidity providers establish early reference boundaries.
3. **Key Intraday Liquidity Pools:** Prior day high (PDH), prior day low (PDL), overnight/pre-open extremes, and round psychological strikes where stop-loss orders cluster.

## Reading IB Breakouts vs. IB Fades

| Session Structure | Typical Behaviour | Execution Approach |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **IB Breakout With Acceptance** | Price breaks above IB high (or below IB low) and holds on pullbacks | Trade trend continuation in the breakout direction with a stop inside the IB |
| **IB Sweep & Reversal** | Price spikes past PDH/PDL or IB edge, fails to hold, and reverses inside | Look for reversal or range-rotation setups back toward mid-IB / POC |
| **Narrow IB Compression** | Tight first-hour range on calm days | Watch for mid-morning or afternoon expansion once liquidity is cleared |

## Key Takeaways

* Avoid overtrading inside the middle of a wide Initial Balance where risk-to-reward is poor.
* Let **PDH, PDL, and the 10:15 AM IB boundary** frame where stop clusters sit before choosing a directional or mean-reverting setup.
