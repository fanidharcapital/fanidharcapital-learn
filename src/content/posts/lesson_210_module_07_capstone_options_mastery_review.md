## 1. Complete Pre-Trade Options Checklist

Run every option idea through these **8 non-negotiable gates**:

1.  **Chart Structure & Bias:** Is there a clear trend/range setup, key support/resistance level, and structural invalidation level from Modules 04–05?
2.  **Underlying, Expiry & Settlement Check:** Have you confirmed whether it's an **Index Option** (cash-settled) or **Stock Option** (physically settled), checked `DTE`/expiry day, and verified **F&O ban / earnings / delivery cutoffs**?
3.  **Option-Chain & Sentiment Context:** Where do major **Call OI / Put OI** walls and **`ΔOI`** shifts sit, and what does **`PCR`** / market context suggest—without treating OI as a standalone signal?
4.  **Volatility & Time-Decay Check:** How do **`IV` / `India VIX`** and remaining **time to expiry (`Theta`)** affect your position—are premiums inflated ahead of an event (`IV crush` risk) or decaying fast near expiry?
5.  **Strike & Moneyness Selection:** Does your chosen strike (`ITM`, `ATM`, or `OTM`) balance **price sensitivity, extrinsic decay, and probability** with your directional or range thesis?
6.  **Liquidity & Spread Check:** Are **Volume, OI, and order-book depth** healthy with a tight **bid-ask spread** so you can enter and exit cleanly via **limit / marketable limit** orders?
7.  **Breakeven & Cost/STT Math:** Have you calculated expiry breakeven (`Strike + Premium` for Calls; `Strike - Premium` for Puts) while factoring in **brokerage, STT, exchange/SEBI fees, stamp duty, GST, and slippage**?
8.  **Position Sizing & Risk Limits:** Is lot count sized to your **`1R` risk budget** (and margin/cash buffer for option selling), with clear stop-loss and daily loss rules before execution?

## 2. Module 07 Mastery Verification

Before stepping into **Module 08**, check that you can answer these five capstone questions clearly:

1.  **Rights vs. Obligations:** Why is a **Call Buyer's** loss capped at premium paid while a **Call Seller's** upside loss risk is open-ended?
2.  **Value & Decay:** How do **Intrinsic Value** and **Extrinsic Value** split an option's premium, and which one decays to `₹0` at expiry?
3.  **IV Crush:** Why can a buyer of a Call option lose money even when the underlying stock rises after an earnings announcement?
4.  **Option Chain (`OI` & `PCR`):** How do high Call OI and high Put OI relate to resistance and support zones, and why is **PCR** used as a contrarian indicator?
5.  **Settlement & Taxes:** Why must traders watch physical settlement rules on **stock options** and understand how **STT** differs between squaring off and `ITM` exercise/settlement?
