In **Lesson #283**, we examined earnings season and active institutional re-rating. In **Lesson #284**, we look at **mechanical institutional money**: **Index Rebalancing & Passive Flows** driven by index funds and `ETFs` tracking domestic (`Nifty`, `Sensex`) and global (`MSCI`, `FTSE`) indices.

---

## 1. How Passive Flows Work Mechanically

*   **Zero Discretion, Pure Weight Tracking:** Passive `ETFs` and index mutual funds are mandated to mirror their benchmark index weights closely to minimize **tracking error**.
*   **Periodic Index Reviews:** When index providers announce **stock inclusions, exclusions, free-float weight updates, or capping adjustments**, passive funds must buy the added/up-weighted stocks and sell the deleted/down-weighted stocks—regardless of intraday chart patterns.

---

## 2. Closing-Window Volume Surges & Post-Rebalance Behavior

*   **Concentrated Execution Near the Close:** To match official index closing prices on the **effective rebalancing date**, passive desks execute huge block/market orders during the **final `30` minutes (`3:00–3:30 PM`)**, producing massive volume spikes and sharp closing ticks.
*   **Avoid Confusing Mechanical Flows With New Fundamentals:** A stock often rallies *ahead* of its effective inclusion date as active traders front-run passive buying, then experiences **short-term mean reversion or consolidation** right after passive funds finish buying.

---

## 3. Key Takeaways

*   **Index rebalancing** triggers forced, mechanical buying and selling by passive `ETFs` and index funds around effective dates.
*   Expect **abnormal closing volume spikes** on rebalance days and separate one-day passive liquidity from genuine multi-month fundamental trends.
