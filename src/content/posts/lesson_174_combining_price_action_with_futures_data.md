## 1. The Three-Layer Futures Confluence Stack

Avoid cluttering your screen with dozens of oscillators. Use a strict **top-down hierarchy**:

1.  **Layer 1 — Price Action & Market Structure (Primary Trigger):**
    *   Trend direction (HH/HL or LH/LL), key support/resistance zones, breakout/retest structure, and exact invalidation level.
    *   *Rule:* **No trade exists without Layer 1.** Never buy or sell solely because OI or basis moved.
2.  **Layer 2 — Open Interest & Volume (Participation Filter):**
    *   Check whether a breakout or trend move is backed by **fresh buildup** (rising price + rising OI for longs; falling price + rising OI for shorts) or merely **short covering / long unwinding** (falling OI).
    *   *Rule:* Be selective with breakouts into resistance that show **falling OI** (short covering) without follow-through volume.
3.  **Layer 3 — Basis & Carry Context (Distortion & Crowding Check):**
    *   Check whether futures premium or discount is within normal carry range or stretched due to **upcoming dividends, earnings events, or expiry-week rollovers**.
    *   *Rule:* Adjust expectations during expiry week or ex-dividend windows so carry/rollover shifts aren't mistaken for organic trend strength.

---

## 2. Example Confluence Walkthrough

*   **Bullish Continuation Setup:** Spot/futures breaks out above a multi-day resistance zone on strong volume (**Layer 1**), near-month OI expands steadily showing fresh **Long Buildup** (**Layer 2**), and futures basis holds a healthy, non-distorted carry premium well ahead of expiry (**Layer 3**) $\rightarrow$ **High-quality setup sized via `1R`**.
*   **Low-Conviction Warning:** Price pokes briefly above resistance on average volume (**Layer 1 weak**), OI drops sharply indicating **Short Covering** (**Layer 2 weak**), and the move occurs on expiry afternoon during rollover squaring (**Layer 3 distorted**) $\rightarrow$ **Stand aside**.

---

## 3. Key Takeaways

*   Keep **Price Structure** as Layer 1 (decision maker), **Open Interest/Volume** as Layer 2 (participation check), and **Basis/Carry** as Layer 3 (context/distortion filter).
*   Confluence improves trade selection by filtering out short-covering traps and expiry/dividend distortions.
