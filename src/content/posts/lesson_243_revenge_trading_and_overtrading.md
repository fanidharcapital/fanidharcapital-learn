In **Lesson #242**, we covered fear, greed, and FOMO. In **Lesson #243**, we look at two of the fastest account-killers in active trading: **Revenge Trading** and **Overtrading**—especially during choppy or red sessions.

![Educational Series #243: Revenge Trading & Overtrading](/images/lessons/lesson-243.png)

---

## 1. The Revenge Trading Loop

Revenge trading happens when a loss wounds your ego or frustration builds, prompting an impulsive attempt to **"win back today's red P&L immediately"**:

1.  A planned or unplanned loss occurs.
2.  Frustration or urgency pushes you to jump right back in—often with **larger lot size**, looser stops, or lower-grade setups.
3.  When the market doesn't cooperate, a controlled `-1R` loss balloons into `-3R`, `-5R`, or worse in a single session.

---

## 2. Overtrading, Chop & Friction Drag

*   **Forcing Trades in Noise:** Overtrading occurs when you take mediocre or non-existent setups simply to stay busy, feel "in action," or erase a small loss during sideways/range-bound chop where directional follow-through is weak.
*   **Transaction Costs Compound Every Extra Order:** Frequent low-conviction trades—especially in active options—pile up **brokerage, STT, exchange/SEBI charges, GST, and bid-ask slippage**, steadily bleeding capital even on flat days.

---

## 3. Key Takeaways

*   The market owes you nothing—trying to force back a loss in the same session usually multiplies risk rather than recovering it.
*   **Overtrading** inside low-quality chop drains both **capital** and **transaction friction**, making restraint a core profit driver.
