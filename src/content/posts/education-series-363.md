Module 11 brought together **chart structure and liquidity** with **quantitative risk math and operational SOPs**. Use these prompts to audit how tightly your execution matches your risk rules.

## Concept & Scenario Prompts

1. **Liquidity & Structure:** How do you spot a liquidity sweep or failed breakout trap around prior highs/lows, Value Areas, or Initial Balance boundaries before committing capital?
2. **Expectancy & Drawdown Math:** Why does a deep drawdown demand a disproportionately large percentage recovery, and how does expectancy per rupee risked keep you focused over larger samples?
3. **Sizing, Correlation & Journaling:** How do ATR/volatility sizing, correlation caps, and rule-error tags work together to keep drawdowns controlled and repeatable?

## Quick Self-Check

* If you take three losses in a row, is your next trade **sized by rule**—or driven by an urge to recover quickly?
* Does your journal separate **valid-setup losses** from **rule-violation losses**?
