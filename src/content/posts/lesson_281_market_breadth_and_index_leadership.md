## 1. Broad Rallies vs. Narrow Heavyweight Rallies

Because `Nifty 50` and `Sensex` are **free-float market-capitalization weighted**, a handful of mega-cap stocks can lift the headline index even while most stocks are falling:

*   **Healthy Broad Rally:** Rising index accompanied by strong **Advance-Decline (`A/D`) breadth** across `NSE 500`, expanding stocks above key moving averages (`50-DMA` / `200-DMA`), and participation across `Nifty Midcap` and `Smallcap` indices.
*   **Narrow / Fragile Rally (Breadth Divergence):** Headline index edges higher on `3–5` index heavyweights while **decliners outnumber advancers** and midcaps/smallcaps slip—an early warning that underlying participation is weakening.

---

## 2. Tracking Sector Rotation

Institutional capital rarely exits equities all at once; instead, it **rotates across sectors** as growth, rates, and earnings evolve—such as shifting between **Banks/Financials, IT, Auto, Pharma, FMCG, Metals, and Capital Goods**. Tracking sectoral indices (`Nifty Bank`, `Nifty IT`, `Nifty Auto`, `Nifty Pharma`, etc.) keeps your trades focused on where institutional leadership actually sits.

---

## 3. Key Takeaways

*   **Market breadth (`Advances vs. Declines`)** reveals whether the broader market confirms or diverges from headline index moves.
*   Follow **sector rotation** so your setups align with leading sectors rather than lagging ones.
