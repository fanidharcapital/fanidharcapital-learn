Overnight global cues, earnings releases, and policy headlines frequently cause Nifty, Bank Nifty, or individual stocks to open with a price gap at 9:15 AM IST. A critical skill in market structure is deciding whether a gap is likely to **fill back into prior range** or act as a **breakaway gap** launching a new trend.

## Gap Fill vs. Breakaway Gap Comparison

| Characteristic | Gap-Fill / Fade Context | Breakaway Gap Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Location on Chart** | Inside an established range or right into major resistance/support | Clearing a multi-day/multi-week base, trendline, or key breakout level |
| **Opening Price Action** | Immediate rejection, long upper/lower wick, or failure to hold opening range | Strong hold above/below opening range with shallow pullbacks |
| **Participation & Volume** | Fading momentum or low follow-through after initial spike | Strong volume expansion and sustained directional commitment |

## Practical Rules for Trading Gaps

1. **Never Assume Every Gap Must Fill:** While many minor overnight gaps inside ranges fill intraday, strong breakaway gaps out of major bases can run for days or weeks without filling immediately.
2. **Wait for Early Structure:** Let the first 15–30 minutes show whether buyers or sellers defend the gap zone—especially when trading with reduced size around scheduled macro catalysts.
3. **Use Prior Close & Gap Edge as Reference:** Watch how price reacts when testing the opening range boundary or prior session close; acceptance or rejection tells you which side is in control.

## Key Takeaways

* **Context and follow-through** matter far more than the size of the gap alone.
* Respect breakaway gaps out of major bases instead of reflexively fading every morning gap.
