Welcome to **Module 02: Exchanges & Operations**. This introductory page gives you a clear summary of what **Days 31–60** cover before you dive into exchange workflows.

---

## What You Will Master in Module 02

* **Exchange Architecture & Sessions (`Days 31–38`):** How order matching works, pre-open call auction, continuous trading, closing session, circuit filters, and price bands on `NSE` and `BSE`.
* **Clearing, Settlement & Depositories (`Days 39–46`):** Clearing corporations, novation, `T+1` pay-in/pay-out cycles, `NSDL` and `CDSL` demat mechanics, `TPIN` / `DDPI`, and corporate actions (`dividends`, `splits`, `bonus`, `rights`).
* **Margin, Collateral & Risk Controls (`Days 47–53`):** Peak margin reporting, upfront margin rules, cash vs. non-cash collateral, haircut rules, auto-square-off mechanics, and surveillance frameworks.
* **Operational Hygiene & Module Review (`Days 54–60`):** Contract notes, ledger reconciliation, account security, common operational errors, post-trade audits, and the Module 02 Capstone checklist.
