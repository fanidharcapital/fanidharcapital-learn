In **Lesson #257**, we separated setup quality from execution quality on every logged trade. In **Lesson #258**, we put that journal data to work through **Weekly and Monthly Performance Reviews**—turning raw entries into clear, testable improvements rather than emotional guesses.

![Educational Series #258: Weekly & Monthly Performance Reviews](/images/lessons/lesson-258.png)

---

## 1. Weekly Review: Spot Leaks & Execution Patterns

Once a week (for example, over the weekend when markets are closed), filter your journal across four quick slices:

1.  **Rule Adherence Breakdown:** Compare average `R` and drawdown on **clean rule-following trades** versus **rule-break trades**.
2.  **Setup & Strategy Slice:** Which setups or option structures generated positive `R`, and which ones bled points or credit?
3.  **Time-of-Day & Session Window:** Did your edge perform better in morning trend windows, midday chop, or expiry sessions?
4.  **Top `2` Mistakes & Top `2` Wins:** Write down the two clearest behavioral or execution lessons from the week.

---

## 2. Monthly Review: Sample Edge, Regime Fit & Friction Audit

At month-end, step back across a broader sample:

*   **Net Expectancy & Drawdown:** Check net `R` expectancy, win rate vs. average win/loss, maximum peak-to-trough drawdown, and total transaction friction (`brokerage, STT, exchange fees, GST, slippage`).
*   **Regime Alignment:** Did `India VIX` / trend conditions favor your primary playbook, or did a regime shift cause losses?
*   **Adopt One or Two Concrete Rule Tweaks:** Avoid rewriting your entire system every weekend after a few trades. Pick **one or two specific, measurable adjustments** (such as *"no new entries after 2:00 PM on choppy days"* or *"cap correlated index spreads at 2R total heat"*) and test them over the next sample.

---

## 3. Key Takeaways

*   **Weekly reviews** catch execution leaks, timing issues, and emotional triggers early; **monthly reviews** evaluate sample expectancy, drawdown, friction, and regime fit.
*   Make **focused, rule-based refinements** over meaningful samples rather than reactive overhauls after every single session.

2.  What is the risk of changing five trading rules at once after a single tough week?
3.  What **three big-picture metrics** should you check during a monthly performance review?
