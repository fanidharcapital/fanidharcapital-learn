Before moving into advanced synthesis, test your command of how Indian equity and derivatives markets actually operate behind the screen—from order routing and matching to clearing and settlement.

## Concept & Scenario Prompts

1. **Primary vs. Secondary Markets:** When a company issues new shares to the public or institutions, where is capital raised—and where does subsequent share trading among investors take place?
2. **Order Matching & Price Discovery:** How do price-time priority and call auctions (such as pre-open and closing sessions) establish fair reference prices?
3. **Clearing & T+1 Settlement:** What role does a clearing corporation play after a trade matches, and what is the practical timeline of **T+1** settlement for funds and demat securities?
4. **Depositories & Demat Safeguards:** How do CDSL and NSDL record electronic holdings via ISINs, and how do DDPI / TPIN / margin-pledge workflows work?

## Quick Self-Check

* Can you explain the difference between **price-time priority** and **pro-rata** matching without confusing exchange mechanics with broker order display?
* Do you know why **margin segregation, client upstreaming, and T+1 settlement** matter for everyday account hygiene?
