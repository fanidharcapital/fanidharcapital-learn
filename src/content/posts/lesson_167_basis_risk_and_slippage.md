## 1. Why Futures & Spot Diverge Intraday

Even though futures and spot track the same underlying index or stock, they are separate order books with distinct participants:

*   **Basis Fluctuations:** Futures basis expands and contracts intraday due to derivatives order flow, hedging/arbitrage activity, rollover shifts, and sentiment—meaning a breakout or support touch on the **futures chart** may not trigger at the exact same moment or level on the **spot chart**.
*   **Hedging Imperfection (Basis Risk):** If you hedge a cash/spot portfolio using futures (or vice versa), any unpredictable change in basis between entry and exit creates **basis risk**—the hedge may over- or under-hedge part of the move before expiry.

---

## 2. How Basis & Slippage Interact on Stops and Entries

Combining basis swings with **slippage** (Lesson #163) creates two common traps:

1.  **Stop-Loss Mismatches:** Placing a stop based on a spot level while trading the futures contract (or vice versa) without accounting for basis can get you stopped out early—or let a futures move run further past your spot reference than expected.
2.  **Wide Spreads & Fast Tape:** When volatility spikes or liquidity thins, bid-ask spreads widen and basis jumps faster, causing stop/market fills to slip away from your planned level.

---

## 3. Clean Execution Habits

*   **Pick One Primary Reference Chart:** Decide whether your entry, invalidation/stop-loss, and target rules are defined on the **futures chart** or the **spot chart**, and stay consistent.
*   **Use Limit / Stop-Limit Discipline Where Appropriate:** Be mindful of bid-ask depth and fast-tape slippage when entering or exiting leveraged lots.

---

## 4. Key Takeaways

*   **Basis risk** arises because futures and spot can diverge before expiry due to order flow, hedging, and basis fluctuations.
*   Unchecked basis differences plus **slippage** can distort stop-loss levels, hedge precision, and net P&L.
*   Keep your **chart reference and risk rules consistent** and account for spread/slippage conditions.
