Across Lessons `#301` through `#329`, **Module 11 (Advanced Concepts)** connected three layers of serious trading: **how price and liquidity move on charts**, **how volatility and options Greeks behave across regimes**, and **how math, risk rules, and SOPs keep execution disciplined over time**.

## Integrating All Three Parts of Module 11

1. **Structure & Liquidity First (`#301`–`#310`):** Read higher-timeframe trend vs. range, watch liquidity sweeps and BOS/CHoCH around key levels (PDH/PDL, Initial Balance, Value Areas, VWAP), and avoid chasing traps inside chop.
2. **Volatility & Options Architecture (`#311`–`#320`):** Compare HV vs. IV, IVR/IVP, skew, and term structure to match spreads, calendars, or directional setups to the regime—while respecting gamma, theta/vega trade-offs, and expiry pin risk.
3. **Quantitative & Operational Discipline (`#321`–`#329`):** Size trades using risk math and ATR/volatility, protect against drawdowns and hidden correlation, execute cleanly, journal every trade with clear tags, and follow your written playbook.

## Module 11 Capstone Self-Check

* Before entering a trade, can you state **the market structure context**, **the volatility/option choice**, **your exact stop and rupee risk**, and **why the trade fits your playbook**?
* Are you evaluating your edge over **meaningful samples net of costs** rather than reacting to single wins or losses?
* Do you have a written **pre-market and post-trade SOP** you actually follow?

With **Module 11 (`#301`–`#330`)** complete, you are ready for the final module of the 12-week curriculum: **Module 12 (`#331`–`#365`)**.
