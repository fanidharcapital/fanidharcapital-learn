In **Lesson #246**, we covered recency bias and overconfidence across streaks. In **Lesson #247**, we address a quieter performance drain: **Decision Fatigue** and **Screen Addiction**—and how structured routines keep your mind sharp.

![Educational Series #247: Decision Fatigue & Screen Addiction](/images/lessons/lesson-247.png)

---

## 1. How Constant Screen Watching & Fatigue Hurt Judgment

*   **Decision Fatigue:** Every chart check, order tweak, and "should I hold or exit?" question consumes mental bandwidth. As fatigue builds later in a session, patience drops and impulsive or sloppy decisions rise.
*   **Screen Addiction & Tick Noise:** Staring continuously at flashing ticks and `1`-minute noise turns minor fluctuations into emotional emergencies, encouraging unnecessary entries and constant tinkering.

---

## 2. Time-Blocked Routines & Screen Boundaries

Structure your trading day into clear, bounded blocks:

1.  **Pre-Market Routine:** Review key levels, trend/range context, volatility/event calendar, and watchlist **before** the bell—no frantic guessing at the open.
2.  **Live-Market Execution Window:** Trade only pre-defined setups and alerts; step away from screens between setups or during quiet midday chop when no valid signal exists.
3.  **Post-Market Debrief & Screen Cutoff:** Log trades, grade rule adherence, and plan tomorrow—then **close the trading terminal** so your mind can recover.

---

## 3. Key Takeaways

*   Continuous screen watching and **decision fatigue** degrade judgment and encourage impulsive clicks.
*   Clear **pre-market, live-market, and post-market blocks**—paired with intentional step-away breaks—preserve clarity.
