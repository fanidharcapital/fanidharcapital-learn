A complete **personal trading playbook** does not need to be fifty pages long—it needs to be clear, practical, and easy to follow before, during, and after every market session.

## Example One-Page Playbook Structure

1. **Weekly & Pre-Market Prep (SOP 1):** Check macro/flow cues, key levels (PDH/PDL, structure, VWAP), scheduled events, and volatility regime (VIX / IVR).
2. **Setup & Regime Filter (SOP 2):** Trade only approved setups that match the current trend/range and volatility environment—and honor explicit *no-trade* rules.
3. **Entry, Stop & Position Sizing (SOP 3):** Enter only on confirmed trigger, place structural stop immediately, and size quantity by fixed rupee risk and ATR/volatility.
4. **In-Trade Management & Exit (SOP 4):** Follow pre-set target/trail or adjustment rules without moving stops wider or averaging down losers; respect daily loss limits.
5. **Journal & Review (SOP 5):** Tag every trade by setup, regime, and rule adherence, then review weekly to refine process—never mid-emotion.

## Key Execution Lessons

* Your playbook is a **living operating checklist**, not a static document to file away and forget.
* Consistent execution of a focused playbook builds real compounding discipline over time.
