Execution details—bid-ask spreads, order types, strike liquidity, and trade frequency—directly shape how much of your gross edge survives as net return.

## Case Walkthrough: Clean Execution vs. High-Friction Churn

* **High-Friction Approach:** Firing market orders on wide-spread option strikes during fast ticks, entering illiquid far-OTM contracts, and taking marginal low-conviction trades that rack up repeated brokerage, statutory charges, and slippage.
* **Disciplined Execution Approach:** Selecting liquid strikes/contracts, using sensible limit buffers rather than chasing erratic spikes, sizing cleanly, and taking only planned setups that clear your quality bar.

## Key Execution Lessons

* **Protect Every Basis Point of Fill Quality:** Slippage and transaction costs act like a silent tax on every entry and exit.
* **Trade Fewer, Higher-Quality Setups:** Cutting low-edge noise lowers friction and sharpens focus.
