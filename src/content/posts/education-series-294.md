Because India imports over 80% of its crude oil requirements, an overnight geopolitical spike in Brent crude paired with a stronger US Dollar Index (DXY) creates an immediate cross-asset shock across Indian equities, currency, and institutional flows.

## Case Setup: Brent Crude Jumps 6% & USD/INR Breaks Resistance

Suppose geopolitical tension pushes Brent crude from $74 to $79 overnight while US Treasury yields rise, causing USD/INR to gap higher at 9:00 AM IST.

### How Institutional Flows & Sectors Diverge

| Market Variable | Overnight / Opening Shift | Institutional & Sector Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **FII Cash & Index Futures** | FIIs sell ₹3,500+ crore in cash and add index shorts | Large-cap index heavyweights face opening gap-down pressure |
| **DII Cash Absorption** | Domestic mutual funds deploy ₹2,800+ crore on dips | Cushion prevents panic cascade and stabilizes Nifty near support |
| **Crude Input Users** | Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs), Aviation, Paints, Tyres | Immediate margin-compression selling; high-volume breakdowns |
| **Export / USD Earners** | Tier-1 IT Services & Export Pharma | Relative strength leaders; rupee depreciation boosts INR revenue |

## Spotting the Flow Inflection Point

1. **Shock Phase (Days 1–2):** Headline index gaps down; FII selling dominates opening liquidity while crude-sensitive stocks underperform sharply.
2. **Absorption Phase (Days 3–4):** Brent crude stalls below resistance, USD/INR stops making new highs, and Nifty forms a higher low intraday despite negative morning headlines.
3. **Inflection Phase (Day 5+):** FII index short-covering begins as DII buying holds the floor, triggering a sharp reversal in beaten-down domestic cyclicals.

## Execution Takeaways From This Case

* **Never Treat All Sectors Equally During a Macro Shock:** Pair-trading or rotating toward **IT and Pharma** during USD/INR spikes protects capital better than blindly averaging down in OMCs or aviation.
* **Watch When Bad News Stops Pushing Price Lower:** When crude rises further or FIIs report another selling day, yet Nifty refuses to break the prior day's low, institutional absorption is near completion.
