When the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) shifts either its policy stance or banking-system liquidity operations (such as CRR adjustments or Variable Rate Repo auctions), equity markets react not just to the headline repo rate, but to the **forward path of funding costs**.

## Case Setup: Liquidity Relief Without an Immediate Repo Cut

Consider a classic Indian market scenario: inflation moderates toward the 4% target band, the MPC holds the repo rate steady, but announces a **50 bps CRR reduction** (or shifts stance from *withdrawal of accommodation* to *neutral*), injecting over ₹1 lakh crore of durable liquidity into the banking system.

### Step-by-Step Cross-Market Transmission

1. **Bond Market Reaction (First 5 Minutes):** The 10-year benchmark G-Sec yield drops 8–12 bps immediately, signalling lower wholesale borrowing costs for banks and NBFCs.
2. **Initial Index Whip (10:00–10:20 AM IST):** Nifty Bank spikes on the headline, pulls back to test VWAP as short-term traders book profits, and holds above its pre-policy opening range.
3. **Institutional Rotation (10:30 AM–3:30 PM IST):**
   * **NBFCs & Housing Finance Companies** lead percentage gains because lower money-market yields directly expand Net Interest Margins (NIMs).
   * **PSU & Private Banks** break above multi-day resistance on above-average volume as treasury mark-to-market gains improve.
   * **Nifty Auto & Nifty Realty** follow with strong afternoon accumulation as lower EMI expectations support demand visibility.

## Execution Takeaways From This Case

* **Watch the 10-Year G-Sec Yield Alongside Bank Nifty:** Equity breakouts backed by falling bond yields have far higher follow-through than isolated equity spikes.
* **Differentiate Funding-Sensitive Winners:** Pure-play NBFCs and wholesale-funded lenders often move faster than deposit-heavy banks when system liquidity eases.
* **Trade the Post-10:30 AM Retest:** Waiting for the first 30-minute range breakout avoids early slippage while capturing the institutional afternoon trend.
