Welcome to **Module 06: Futures Market**. This introductory page gives you a clear summary of what **Days 151–180** cover across stock and index futures on Indian exchanges.

---

## What You Will Master in Module 06

* **Futures Contract Mechanics (`Days 151–158`):** Underlying index vs. single-stock futures, contract specifications, lot sizes, tick size, expiry cycles, and physical vs. cash settlement basics.
* **Pricing, Basis & Cost of Carry (`Days 159–166`):** Spot vs. futures price relationship, premium/discount (basis), cost of carry, dividend adjustments, and convergence toward expiry.
* **Margin, Leverage & MTM (`Days 167–173`):** Initial margin, exposure margin, daily mark-to-market (`MTM`) cash flows, leverage magnification of gains and losses, and shortfall risks.
* **Rollovers, Flows & Module Review (`Days 174–180`):** Rollover spread, expiry-week dynamics, futures open interest alongside price, common futures pitfalls, case studies, and the Module 06 Capstone checklist.
