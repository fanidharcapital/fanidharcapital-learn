## 1. Master Pre-Trade Checklist (Run Before Every Options Order)

### Step 1: Market Regime & Greeks Alignment
*   **Price Structure:** Is the underlying **trending** (breakout/pullback), **range-bound** (between clear support and resistance), or **coiled for a catalyst**?
*   **Volatility (`IV` / `India VIX`):** Is `IV` **low/normal** (favoring debit spreads, calendars, or long volatility) or **elevated** (favoring credit spreads, Iron Condors, or Iron Butterflies)?
*   **Net Greeks Check:** Do my net **Delta, Gamma, Theta, and Vega** actually match how I expect price, time, and volatility to behave?

### Step 2: Strike Placement, `1R` Sizing & Liquidity
*   **Structural Anchoring:** Are short strikes buffered outside clear chart support/resistance, and are debit spread targets realistic before expiry?
*   **Defined Max Loss $\le 1R$:** Have I sized my **lot count** by worst-case defined loss (`1R`) rather than low margin, and checked correlated portfolio exposure?
*   **Liquidity & Margin:** Are bid-ask spreads tight, and am I executing all legs together via a **limit spread/basket order**?

### Step 3: Exit, Adjustment & Expiry Plan
*   **Profit & Stop Rules Written Down:** Do I have a clear **profit target** (e.g., `50%–75%` of credit or technical target) and a **hard stop/invalidation trigger**?
*   **Expiry & Settlement Check:** Have I planned my exit well ahead of expiry-day **Gamma acceleration** and NSE/BSE **physical settlement** deadlines on stock options?

## 2. Key Takeaways

*   **Module 08 Mastery:** Successful options trading is never about memorizing exotic strategy names—it is about matching **Greeks and payoff shape** to the **current market/volatility regime** while enforcing **defined `1R` risk** on every single trade.
