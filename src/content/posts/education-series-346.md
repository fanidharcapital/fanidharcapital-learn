Two traders can both build positive expectancy over 100 trades while experiencing completely different emotional ride-alongs along the way.

## Comparing Two 100-Trade Profiles (Risking ₹5,000 per Trade = $1\text{R}$)

* **System A (Breakout / Trend Follower — $40\%$ Win Rate, $2.0\text{R}$ Average Win):**
  * 40 wins $\times$ ₹10,000 = +₹4,00,000; 60 losses $\times$ ₹5,000 = -₹3,00,000.
  * Net gross gain = **+₹1,00,000** (**+$0.20\text{R}$ per trade**), even though 6 out of 10 trades lose.
* **System B (Structured Swing / Spreads — $60\%$ Win Rate, $0.85\text{R}$ Average Win):**
  * 60 wins $\times$ ₹4,250 = +₹2,55,000; 40 losses $\times$ ₹5,000 = -₹2,00,000.
  * Net gross gain = **+₹55,000** before costs—requiring tight control on losing trades so a few bigger losses do not erase the higher hit rate.

## Key Execution Lessons

* **Win Rate Alone Does Not Define a System:** A $40\%$ win rate with disciplined stops and $2.0\text{R}$ average winners can outperform a $60\%$ win rate that lets occasional losses run wide.
* **Always Subtract Real Trading Costs:** Brokerage, statutory charges, and slippage hit high-frequency or lower-payoff systems harder per rupee of gross profit.
