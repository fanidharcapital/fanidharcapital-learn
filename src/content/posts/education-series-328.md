Two traders can take the exact same setup and end up with noticeably different results based on **execution quality**. Wide bid-ask spreads, illiquid strikes, impulsive market orders, and frequent overtrading quietly erode net expectancy.

## Practical Execution Habits

1. **Favor Limit Orders With Sensible Buffers:** Especially in options or fast-moving stocks, using limit orders (or carefully bounded prices) helps avoid paying wide spreads during sudden ticks.
2. **Check Liquidity & Spread Width First:** Deep OTM strikes, far expiries, or low-volume contracts often have wide bid-ask gaps that hurt entry and exit fills immediately.
3. **Slice Larger Orders When Needed:** When scaling into or out of larger quantities or multi-leg option structures, entering cleanly rather than chasing erratic ticks reduces adverse slippage.
4. **Account for All Trading Costs:** Factor in brokerage, exchange transaction fees, STT, stamp duty, GST, and slippage—high-frequency churning in thin-edge setups can easily turn gross gains into net losses.

## Key Takeaways

* Good execution protects your **payoff ratio and expectancy** on every single trade.
* Liquid instruments, clean pricing discipline, and controlled trade frequency keep friction from eating your edge.
