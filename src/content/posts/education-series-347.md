Losing streaks happen to every trading method when market regimes shift. What separates a temporary dip from a painful multi-month hole is **how quickly you cap the drawdown and scale down size** while waiting for clarity.

## Case Walkthrough: A Choppy Two-Week Regime Shift

* **Starting Account:** ₹10,00,000 with a standard risk budget of ₹10,000 ($1\%$) per trade.
* **The Streak:** Six losses in eight sessions during a whipsaw transition pull the account down **-6%** (to ₹9,40,000).
* **Disciplined Response:** Instead of doubling size to "make it back in two trades," the trader enforces a **half-size rule** (₹5,000 risk per trade) until setups regain traction and account equity stabilizes.
* **Why This Matters:** Keeping losses shallow prevents a -6% dip from snowballing into a -25% or -35% drawdown—which would require **+33.3% to +53.8%** just to recover.

## Key Execution Lessons

* **Never Scale Up During a Losing Streak:** Increasing size after losses is one of the fastest paths to deep drawdowns.
* **Use Tiered Size Reduction as an Automatic Circuit Breaker:** Cutting size when down from peak equity protects both capital and composure.
