## 1. The Four Core Data Points (OHLC)

Every candlestick plots exactly four prices recorded during its chosen timeframe (e.g., 9:15 AM to 3:30 PM on a Daily NSE candle):

*   **Open (O):** The first traded price when the session or interval begins. On NSE/BSE daily charts, this reflects the equilibrium price discovered during the 9:00–9:15 AM pre-open session.
*   **High (H):** The highest price buyers were willing to pay during that period before sellers stepped in.
*   **Low (L):** The lowest price sellers were willing to accept during that period before buyers stepped in.
*   **Close (C):** The final price at the end of the interval. Because institutional players and algorithmic books settle positions into the close, the **Close** carries the highest psychological and structural weight of all four prints.

---

## 2. Real Bodies vs. Shadows (Wicks)

Once OHLC data is plotted, two distinct visual parts emerge—each representing a different phase of the buyer–seller auction:

| Candlestick Part | Formula / Location | Behavioral Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bullish Real Body (Green)** | $\text{Close} > \text{Open}$ | Buyers absorbed available supply and pushed price higher from open to close. |
| **Bearish Real Body (Red)** | $\text{Close} < \text{Open}$ | Sellers overwhelmed demand and drove price lower from open to close. |
| **Upper Shadow (Upper Wick)** | $\text{High} - \max(\text{Open}, \text{Close})$ | **Supply Rejection:** Price rallied intraday, hit overhead selling, and was forced back down before the close. |
| **Lower Shadow (Lower Wick)** | $\min(\text{Open}, \text{Close}) - \text{Low}$ | **Demand Rejection:** Price dropped intraday, found aggressive buying interest, and recovered before the close. |

---

## 3. Reading Proportions: What Each Print Reveals

A candle's shape is not random art—it is the footprint of order-flow battle across three dimensions:

### A. Body-to-Range Ratio (Conviction vs. Hesitation)
*   **Large Real Body (>70% of total High–Low range):** Strong directional consensus. One side dominated the entire session with minimal pushback.
*   **Small Real Body (<25% of total High–Low range):** Equilibrium or stall. Despite intraday movement, neither buyers nor sellers held net control by the close.

### B. Close Location Value (Who Won the Final Bell?)
Where the Close sits inside the total High–Low range tells you who enters the next candle with momentum:
*   **Close in the Top 25% of Range:** Buyers controlled the finish; momentum favors follow-through higher.
*   **Close in the Bottom 25% of Range:** Sellers controlled the finish; momentum favors follow-through lower.
*   **Close in the Middle 50%:** Unresolved battle; wait for the next candle before committing capital.

### C. Asymmetric Shadows (Failed Auctions)
*   A **long lower shadow** after a decline shows sellers tried to break price down, failed, and got trapped as buyers stepped in.
*   A **long upper shadow** after an advance shows buyers chased higher prices, ran into a wall of supply, and got trapped by the close.

## 4. Practical Application on NSE / BSE

1.  **Never Judge an Unclosed Candle:** During live market hours (e.g., at 11:30 AM on a Daily chart), a candle can look like a massive green breakout bar and still finish at 3:30 PM as a red candle with a long upper wick. Always wait for the candle close to confirm the final OHLC print.
2.  **Pay Attention to the 3:00–3:30 PM NSE Window:** Institutional flows, mutual fund execution, and intraday square-offs heavily shape the final Daily close on NSE stocks. A stock that holds near its intraday high into 3:30 PM signals genuine institutional accumulation.
3.  **Compare Body Size to the Last 5–10 Bars:** Context starts with immediate neighbors. A medium green body after five tiny bodies is a meaningful shift; the same green body after three giant bars represents deceleration.

---

## 5. Key Takeaways

*   Every candle distills an entire auction into **Open, High, Low, and Close (OHLC)**.
*   **Real bodies** show net directional commitment; **shadows (wicks)** show rejected price extremes.
*   The **Close Location** within the High–Low range reveals which side won the final battle before the next period begins.
