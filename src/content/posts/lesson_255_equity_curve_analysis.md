---

## 1. Key Metrics on Any Equity Curve

1.  **Peak-to-Trough Drawdown (`Max DD`):** The largest percentage or `R`-drop from an equity high to a subsequent low before a new high is reached.
2.  **Recovery Factor:** Measures how much net profit your system generates per unit of worst drawdown:
    $$\text{Recovery Factor} = \frac{\text{Net Profit}}{\text{Maximum Drawdown}}$$
    *(Higher recovery factors indicate smoother, more resilient equity growth.)*
3.  **Underwater Duration:** How many trading days or weeks it takes to recover from a drawdown back to a new equity peak—helping prepare you psychologically for normal slow periods.

---

## 2. Objective Rules for Scaling Position Size Up or Down

*   **When to Scale Up (`+0.25%` `1R` step):** Only after reaching a **new equity high**, maintaining strong **process/rule-adherence scores** over a solid sample (e.g., `30–50+` trades), and confirming calm emotional execution.
*   **When to Scale Down (`Cut 1R by 50%`):** When your equity curve drops below a predefined drawdown threshold (such as `-5R` or `-6%`) or shows a persistent downturn—reducing size preserves capital and confidence until your curve stabilizes and climbs back.

---

## 3. Key Takeaways

*   Tracking **peak-to-trough drawdowns, Recovery Factor, and underwater duration** keeps expectations realistic during normal variance.
*   Scale position size **up at new equity highs** and **down during deep drawdowns** using pre-set rules rather than emotion—ready for **Part 3: Trade Journaling, Backtesting & Edge Validation**.
