## 1. What Is India VIX?

*   **India VIX (India Volatility Index)** is computed by NSE from the order book of **Nifty index options** across near- and mid-term expiries.
*   It expresses the market's expectation of **annualized volatility over the next 30 calendar days** in percentage terms.
*   **Inverse Tendency With Equity Markets:** Historically, India VIX tends to **rise during sharp market sell-offs and uncertainty** (when demand for protective Puts spikes) and **drift lower during steady, calm bull trends**.

---

## 2. How Volatility Regimes Shape Option Trading

| Volatility Regime | Typical Market Behavior | Option Pricing & Sizing Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Low VIX Regime** | Tight daily ranges, slow grinding trends, calm sentiment. | Option premiums are **relatively cheap**; option buyers face slow movement/theta drag unless breakout momentum arrives, while option sellers collect smaller premiums. |
| **Normal VIX Regime** | Healthy two-way swings, clean technical breakouts and pullbacks. | Balanced extrinsic premiums; both structured directional option buying and disciplined selling operate cleanly. |
| **High / Spiking VIX Regime** | Wide intraday ranges, sharp gaps, headline/event turbulence. | Option premiums are **expensive**; daily point swings are large, requiring **wider stops, smaller lot sizes**, and strict risk limits (especially for option writers). |

---

## 3. Practical Rules for Using India VIX

1.  **VIX Is a Regime Filter, Not a Standalone Buy/Sell Signal:** A low VIX does not mean "short the market tomorrow," and a high VIX does not mean "buy blindly." Use VIX to **calibrate position size, stop width, and premium expectations**.
2.  **Scale Down Lot Count When VIX Spikes:** Because wider daily index ranges increase point risk per lot, keeping your **`1R` risk budget constant** automatically means trading **fewer lots** during high-VIX turbulence.

---

## 4. Key Takeaways

*   **India VIX** measures expected **30-day annualized Nifty volatility** derived from live Nifty option prices.
*   High VIX regimes bring **wider daily ranges and richer option premiums**, demanding **smaller lot sizes and tighter risk discipline**.
*   Treat India VIX as a **market-regime and risk-calibration gauge** alongside price structure.
