## 1. Why Daily Flow Headlines Often Trap Traders

Every evening, social media and market commentary highlight single-day `FII` net buy/sell figures or participant `F&O` numbers. Over-reacting to every daily print leads to constant bias flipping because single-day numbers frequently reflect:

*   One-off **block/bulk deals, `QIPs`, or `IPO` anchors** that distort headline daily totals.
*   **Expiry-day rollovers, index arbitrage (`cash-futures`), or routine portfolio hedging** rather than directional conviction.
*   **Passive index rebalancing** concentrated in a single closing auction.

---

## 2. A Three-Filter Confluence Check

Before letting institutional data influence your trading plan, require alignment across three lenses:

1.  **Multi-Week Trend (`10–20` Sessions):** Is cumulative cash/institutional activity steadily accumulating or distributing over weeks, not just one afternoon?
2.  **Market Breadth & Sector Leadership:** Are `Advance-Decline` breadth and leading sectors (`Banks`, `IT`, `Auto`, `Capex`, etc.) confirming the move?
3.  **Price Structure as Final Judge:** If headline flows look negative for a day or two while the index holds above key support and higher lows, **trust price structure over evening headlines**.

---

## 3. Key Takeaways

*   Single-day institutional flow prints contain heavy **arbitrage, hedging, and one-off deal noise**.
*   Trust **multi-week flow trends, market breadth, and chart structure**—ready for **Part 3: Integrating Macro & Institutional Cues Into Price Action & Options**.
