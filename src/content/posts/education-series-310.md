Adding a dozen indicators to a chart rarely improves clarity—it usually hides what price is actually doing around key liquidity levels. Lessons `#301` through `#309` come down to one disciplined principle: **keep charts clean so structure, liquidity, and volatility remain unmistakable**.

## Clean Four-Step Chart & Liquidity Workflow

1. **Mark Higher-Timeframe Structure & Key Pools:** Plot major swing highs/lows, prior day high/low (PDH/PDL), and clear Value Area / support-resistance zones before the session starts.
2. **Identify the Active State (Balance vs. Imbalance / Coil):** Note whether price is rotating inside a balanced range, coiling in tight compression, or expanding with a clean BOS/CHoCH.
3. **Watch for Traps & Liquidity Sweeps:** Avoid chasing breakouts right into obvious stop clusters without confirmation; look for sweep-and-reclaim or clean post-sweep continuation.
4. **Check Cross-Asset Context & Keep Execution Simple:** Note Bank Nifty, India VIX, and VWAP context, then execute only when entry, stop-loss, and risk size are clear.

## Part 1 Self-Check Questions

* Can you tell the difference between a normal pullback (**BOS** continuation) and a true structural shift (**CHoCH**) on your chart?
* Do you mark **PDH, PDL, and Initial Balance** boundaries instead of drawing dozens of cluttered lines?
* Are your charts focused on **price, volume/VWAP context, and key levels** rather than conflicting oscillator signals?
