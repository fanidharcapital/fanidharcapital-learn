## 1. Index Options vs. Stock Options at a Glance

| Feature | Index Options (e.g., Nifty / Sensex) | Single-Stock Options (NSE F&O Stocks) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Underlying** | Broad market or sector index (diversified basket of stocks). | Individual listed company stock (exposed to company-specific gaps/earnings). |
| **Expiry Cadence (Current SEBI/Exchange Structure)** | Benchmark indices carry **weekly and monthly expiries** (NSE index weekly/monthly on **Tuesdays**; BSE Sensex weekly/monthly on **Thursdays**; other indices like Bank Nifty/Fin Nifty on **monthly** cycles—subject to exchange holiday shifts). | **Monthly expiry only** (no weekly stock options). |
| **Settlement at Expiry** | **Cash-settled** (net ITM intrinsic value credited/debited in cash). | **Physically settled** (ITM options held through expiry require full share delivery/payment—see **Lesson #205**). |
| **Single-Event Gap Risk** | Lower single-company shock risk (diversified across constituents). | High **earnings, corporate action, and news gap risk**. |
| **Liquidity & Spreads** | Deep liquidity and tight spreads on benchmark ATM/near-OTM strikes. | Liquidity concentrates in top large-cap F&O names and ATM/near-ATM strikes; far-OTM strikes can be very thin. |

*(Always verify the official NSE/BSE expiry calendar for holidays—when an expiry day falls on an exchange holiday, expiry shifts to the preceding trading session.)*

---

## 2. Why Physical Settlement Changes Stock Option Rules

*   In **Index Options**, forgetting to square off an In-The-Money (`ITM`) option at `3:30 PM` on expiry day simply results in cash settlement of the intrinsic value.
*   In **Stock Options**, holding an `ITM` option through expiry triggers **mandatory physical share delivery**—requiring full contract value in cash (for `Long CE` / `Short PE`) or actual demat shares (for `Long PE` / `Short CE`), plus escalating expiry-week delivery margins!

---

## 3. Key Takeaways

*   **Index options** offer **cash settlement**, lower single-stock event risk, and active weekly/monthly liquidity (NSE Tuesday / BSE Thursday cycles for benchmark weekly contracts).
*   **Stock options** expire **monthly**, carry single-company earnings/news risk, and are **physically settled** if held ITM through expiry.
