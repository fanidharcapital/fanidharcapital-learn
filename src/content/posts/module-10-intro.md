Welcome to **Module 10: Institutions & Macro**. This introductory page gives you a clear summary of what **Days 271–300** cover across macroeconomic drivers and institutional flows.

---

## What You Will Master in Module 10

* **Domestic & Global Macro Cues (`Days 271–278`):** `CPI`/`WPI`, `RBI` policy and liquidity, `GDP`/`PMI`/`IIP`, Union Budget and yields, `US Fed` and global yields, crude oil, `USD/INR`, `GIFT Nifty`, and event volatility.
* **Institutional Flows & Breadth (`Days 279–285`):** `FII` vs. `DII` flows, cash vs. derivatives positioning, market breadth and sector rotation, bulk/block deals, earnings guidance, and index rebalancing.
* **Integrating Macro Into Charts (`Days 286–292`):** Top-down alignment from macro to timeframe structure, trading around major events, sector relative strength, event option skew/term structure, and macro risk budgets.
* **Macro Case Studies & Review (`Days 293–300`):** Applied walk-throughs across policy events, currency/crude shifts, earnings reactions, and breadth divergences, plus weekly routines and the Module 10 Capstone checklist.
