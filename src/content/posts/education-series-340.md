Choosing the right option structure starts with checking **where implied volatility sits relative to its own history (IVR / IVP)** alongside trend and range context.

## Cycle 1: Low IVR (< 25%) With Breakout Structure

* **Environment:** Volatility has been compressed for weeks; option premiums are relatively inexpensive.
* **Structure Choice:** A **vertical debit spread** (or backspread where appropriate) aligns with directional momentum without overpaying for time value.

## Cycle 2: High IVR (> 65%) After a Volatility Spike

* **Environment:** A recent macro or market scare inflated IVR and option premiums, and spot is now stabilizing inside a defined range.
* **Structure Choice:** A **defined-risk credit spread or iron condor** (placed with sensible strikes and capped wings) benefits from elevated extrinsic value and potential IV mean reversion—while keeping worst-case loss strictly bounded.

## Key Execution Lessons

* **Match Structure to Both Direction and IVR:** A great directional view can still struggle if you buy expensive options at peak IV or sell cheap options at rock-bottom IV.
* **Always Cap Tail Risk:** Keep protective wings in place on spread structures so unexpected gaps never cause outsized damage.
