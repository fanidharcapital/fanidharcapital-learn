Welcome to **Module 09: Risk & Psychology**. This introductory page gives you a clear summary of what **Days 241–270** cover across emotional discipline, capital protection, and edge validation.

---

## What You Will Master in Module 09

* **Psychology & Cognitive Biases (`Days 241–248`):** Thinking in probabilities over single outcomes, managing fear, greed, `FOMO`, revenge trading, loss aversion, anchoring, recency bias, and decision fatigue.
* **Risk Governance & Sizing (`Days 249–255`):** Drawdown math, fixed fractional / `ATR` / `1R` sizing, portfolio heat and correlation limits, daily/weekly loss caps, overnight/event sizing, and expectancy.
* **Journaling & Edge Validation (`Days 256–262`):** Structured trade journaling, `MFE`/`MAE` and execution grading, weekly/monthly reviews, unbiased backtesting with costs/slippage, and forward testing.
* **Trading Playbook & Module Review (`Days 263–270`):** Building a personal playbook, pre-market and post-market routines, regime adaptation, record-keeping hygiene, turnaround case studies, and the Module 09 Capstone checklist.
