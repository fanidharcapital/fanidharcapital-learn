In **Lesson #171**, we covered physical delivery risk in stock futures. In **Lesson #172**, we examine **F&O Ban Periods and MWPL (Market-Wide Position Limit)**—a key exchange risk-management mechanism on NSE that restricts fresh derivative positions whenever aggregate open interest in a stock becomes overly crowded.

---

## 1. What Is MWPL & When Does a Stock Enter F&O Ban?

*   **Market-Wide Position Limit (MWPL):** The maximum aggregate open interest (across all futures and options contracts of a stock on the exchange) permitted by exchange/SEBI rules, linked to non-promoter/free-float share capital and liquidity criteria.
*   **Triggering the Ban (`95%` Threshold):** When aggregate open interest across the market crosses **`95%` of MWPL**, the exchange places the stock into the **F&O Ban Period** starting the next trading session.
*   **Exiting the Ban (`80%` Threshold):** The stock remains in the ban period until aggregate open interest drops back to **`80%` or below** of MWPL.

---

## 2. What You Can and Cannot Do During F&O Ban

| Action | Allowed During F&O Ban? | Practical Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Opening a New Position** | **No** | Fresh futures or options positions that increase open interest are rejected/penalized. |
| **Adding to an Existing Position** | **No** | You cannot average down or pyramid into an existing F&O trade while the stock is in ban. |
| **Closing / Reducing an Existing Position** | **Yes** | Traders can square off existing positions (reducing open interest). |
| **Rolling Over to Next Month** | **Restricted** | Because opening a new next-month leg adds OI in the banned underlying, normal rollovers are blocked until the stock exits ban. |

---

## 3. Trading Risks Inside F&O Ban Stocks

*   **Short Squeezes & Sharp Volatility:** Because no fresh shorts or longs can enter to provide two-way liquidity, forced unwinding or short covering can produce erratic, jumpy price action.
*   **Penalties for Violations:** Any trade that increases a client's open position during a ban period attracts exchange penalties—always verify your net position before placing orders in banned scrips.

---

## 4. Key Takeaways

*   A stock enters **F&O ban** when market-wide open interest exceeds **`95%` of MWPL**, and exits only after falling to **`80%` or below**.
*   During ban, **only position-reducing (closing) trades are permitted**; fresh positions, additions, and standard rollovers are blocked.
*   Always check the daily **NSE F&O ban list** before planning new stock-futures entries or rollovers.
