## 1. Anatomy of a Box Consolidation

A **Box Consolidation** forms when price oscillates repeatedly between a well-defined **Range Ceiling (Resistance)** and **Range Floor (Support)**:

*   **Minimum Validation:** At least **two clean touches at the ceiling** and **two clean touches at the floor** define a tradable box.
*   **The Three Zones Inside Every Box:**
    1.  **Upper Boundary (Supply Edge):** Top 15%–20% of the box—where rallies stall and sellers defend.
    2.  **Mid-Range ("No-Man's Land"):** The middle 50% of the box—erratic whipsaws, low Reward-to-Risk, and zero structural edge. **Stay out here.**
    3.  **Lower Boundary (Demand Edge):** Bottom 15%–20% of the box—where sell-offs find bids and buyers defend.

---

## 2. Two Distinct Ways to Trade a Range (Never Mix Them Up)

Choose one clear playbook based on the box's width and market context:

| Approach | When to Use It | Entry & Risk Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Playbook A: Boundary Fading (Mean Reversion)** | Wide, multi-week range (`>6%–10%` height) in a neutral/sideways market regime. | Buy rejection candles at the **Range Floor** (stop below floor) targeting the opposite ceiling; sell rejection candles at the **Range Ceiling** (stop above ceiling). |
| **Playbook B: Breakout & Expansion (Trend Continuation)** | Tight consolidation box formed **after an impulsive trend leg** (accumulation/distribution pause). | Ignore mid-range swings; wait for a **decisive body close outside the box** plus volume/retest confirmation before entering. |

---

## 3. Spotting Failed Breakouts (And Turning Them Into Edge)

Statistically, the majority of initial pokes out of a sideways range fail and snap back inside the box:

*   **Warning Signs of a False Breakout:**
    1.  Price breaks the ceiling on a **narrow candle with a long upper selling wick** and weak NSE volume.
    2.  The very next candle closes **back inside the box**, trapping breakout buyers.
*   **The Failed-Breakout Reversal Trade:** When price pokes above the ceiling, fails, and closes back inside the box on a strong red candle, trapped longs liquidate—frequently driving price all the way across the box to the **opposite Range Floor**.

---

## 4. Checklist for a Confirmed Range Expansion

Before trusting a breakout out of an NSE box consolidation, verify at least **three of these four** conditions:

1.  **Pre-Breakout Tightening (Build-Up):** Price coils tightly near the breakout boundary (forming higher lows against resistance) instead of launching from the very bottom of a wide box.
2.  **Wide-Range Body Close Outside the Box:** At least 60%–70% of the breakout candle's real body closes cleanly beyond the box boundary.
3.  **Volume / Delivery Surge:** Breakout bar shows noticeably higher volume than the 10-day average inside the box.
4.  **Acceptance / Hold Above the Box:** Subsequent candles hold above the broken ceiling (forming a **polarity flip**) rather than immediately collapsing back inside.

---

## 5. Key Takeaways

*   Never trade in the **middle of a box consolidation**—edge exists only at the **boundaries** or on **confirmed expansion** outside the box.
*   When a breakout pokes outside the box and **closes back inside**, expect a fast move toward the opposite boundary as trapped traders unwind.
*   High-probability breakouts show **tight build-up near the edge**, a **decisive body close**, and **volume expansion**.
