Welcome to **Lesson 80** of **Module 03: Fundamental Analysis** (**Lessons 61–90**) in the **Shree Fanidhar Capital Educational Series**.

In **Lesson 79**, we examined **Dividend Metrics** (`Dividend Yield`, `Dividend Payout Ratio`, and `Share Buybacks`). As we wrap up **Lessons 74–81: Financial Ratios & Quality Metrics**, a crucial practical insight stands out: generic ratios like `Inventory Turnover` or `EBITDA Margin` either don't apply or miss the real value drivers in specialized sectors. Today, we break down the core **Sector-Specific Metrics** for **Banking**, **IT Services**, **Retail**, and **Pharmaceuticals**.

---

## 1. Banking & Lending (`NIM`, `CASA`, `GNPA / NNPA`)

In banking, **money is both raw material and finished product**. Deposits are the funding base, loans are the earning assets, and credit risk (`NPAs`) determines whether lending spreads turn into real profit:

| Banking Metric | What It Measures | Key Analytical Takeaway |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Net Interest Margin (`NIM`)** | Spread between interest earned on advances/investments and interest paid on deposits/borrowings, divided by average earning assets. | Reflects lending yield discipline vs. cost of funds; watch for pressure when deposit rates rise faster than loan yields. |
| **`CASA` Ratio** *(Current + Savings Account % of Total Deposits)* | Share of low-cost current and savings deposits in total deposits. | Higher `CASA` typically lowers funding costs and supports sticky deposit franchises—though fierce rate competition can test growth. |
| **`GNPA` & `NNPA` (%)** *(Gross & Net Non-Performing Assets)* | Share of loans overdue/impaired (`GNPA`) and net bad loans after provisions (`NNPA`) as a `%` of advances. | Measures asset quality and provisioning cushion; rising `GNPA` or `slippages` can quickly eat into profits via higher credit costs. |

## 2. IT Services (`Attrition`, `TCV / ACV`)

Indian IT services companies sell skilled human capital, execution reliability, and long-term digital/cloud/transformation contracts. Revenue visibility and delivery stability matter most:

| IT Services Metric | What It Measures | Key Analytical Takeaway |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Attrition Rate (%)** | Percentage of employees leaving the firm over a trailing period (typically LTM or quarterly annualized). | Elevated attrition raises hiring, bench, and training costs—and can risk project execution or client satisfaction. |
| **`TCV` & `ACV`** *(Total Contract Value & Annual Contract Value)* | Total lifetime value of deals won (`TCV`) and annualized revenue value (`ACV`) from signed contracts. | Indicates future revenue pipeline and client demand—always check whether deal wins are converting into actual billed revenue. |

*(Pair `TCV/ACV` and `Attrition` with **Utilization**, **Constant Currency (`CC`) growth**, and **EBIT margin** for a complete IT check.)*

## 3. Retail (`SSSG` / Like-for-Like Growth)

A retail chain can grow total revenue simply by opening new stores every quarter—even if existing stores are slowing down. **Same-Store Sales Growth (`SSSG`)** separates organic store demand from store-count expansion:

* **Same-Store Sales Growth (`SSSG` / Like-for-Like Growth):** Measures revenue growth from **existing stores open for a comparable period** (typically `12+ months`), stripping out new store openings and closures.
* **Why It Matters:** Strong `SSSG` shows genuine customer footfall, brand pull, and basket size—whereas flat or falling `SSSG` masked by rapid new-store rollout can pressure return ratios if store economics weaken.

---

## 4. Pharmaceuticals (`R&D Spend`, `ANDA` Filings & Approvals)

Pharma companies—especially Indian formulation/generics and specialty players—depend on continuous pipeline replenishment to offset price erosion on mature molecules:

| Pharma Metric | What It Measures | Key Analytical Takeaway |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`R&D` Spend (% of Revenue)** | Share of sales reinvested into research & development (generics, complex generics, biosimilars, specialty). | Sustains future product pipeline; check whether `R&D` is expensed cleanly in P&L and backed by realistic commercial payoff. |
| **`ANDA` & Regulatory Pipeline** *(Abbreviated New Drug Applications, e.g., USFDA)* | Filings submitted and approvals received to manufacture and market generic/formulation products. | Drives future launches and market share—but actual earnings depend on competition intensity, pricing, and plant inspection clearance. |

---

## Key Takeaways

* **Use sector-native lenses:** `NIM`, `CASA`, and `GNPA/NNPA` anchor **Banking**; `Attrition` and `TCV` anchor **IT Services**; `SSSG` anchors **Retail**; and `R&D` + `ANDA` pipeline anchor **Pharma**.
* **Never rely on a single metric in isolation:** In banks, pair `NIM` with `GNPA/NNPA` and credit costs; in retail, pair `SSSG` with store economics and cash conversion; in pharma, pair `ANDA` counts with real commercial competitiveness and regulatory compliance.
* **Always benchmark sector metrics against direct industry peers and historical cycles**, not cross-sector averages.
