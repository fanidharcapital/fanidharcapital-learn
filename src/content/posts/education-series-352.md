Holding several positions can feel diversified until a single macro shock or sector rotation hits all of them at once.

## Case Walkthrough: Concentrated Theme + Derivatives Exposure

* **The Setup:** A trader holds long cash shares in three banking/financial stocks, sells puts on a financial index, and carries a directional index position—thinking each trade has its own small risk limit.
* **The Hidden Risk:** All positions share heavy sensitivity to **interest rates, banking liquidity, and index direction**. If yields spike or a sharp index pullback hits, correlated losses and expanding F&O risk compound together rather than offsetting one another.

## Key Execution Lessons

* **Group Correlated Positions Under One Theme Cap:** Treat trades driven by the same sector or macro factor as a combined exposure bucket.
* **Check Total Portfolio Stress Before Adding Size:** Always view open cash and F&O positions together as one portfolio.
