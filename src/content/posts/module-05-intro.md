Welcome to **Module 05: Price Action**. This introductory page gives you a clear summary of what **Days 121–150** cover as you sharpen raw price-action reading.

---

## What You Will Master in Module 05

* **Candles in Context & Momentum (`Days 121–128`):** Reading candle sequences rather than isolated patterns, compression vs. expansion, follow-through candles, and exhaustion clues.
* **Market Structure & Key Levels (`Days 129–136`):** Swing highs/lows, trend shifts, supply/demand zones, opening range behavior, session highs/lows, and multi-timeframe alignment.
* **Entries, Stops & Target Framing (`Days 137–143`):** Pullback vs. breakout entries, structural invalidation stops, logical profit targets, risk-to-reward evaluation, and avoiding mid-range entries.
* **Price Action Execution & Review (`Days 144–150`):** Handling choppy sessions, gap scenarios, execution consistency, chart review drills, case studies, and the Module 05 Capstone checklist.
