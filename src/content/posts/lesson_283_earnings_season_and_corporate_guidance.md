## 1. Beyond Headline Net Profit: Quality & Guidance

Institutions rarely price a stock on a single headline net-profit number alone—especially if profit was boosted by one-off other income or tax adjustments. They focus on three deeper drivers:

1.  **Revenue Growth & Operating Margin Quality:** Is top-line demand growing organically, and are **operating margins (`EBITDA` / `EBIT` margins)** expanding or compressing under cost pressures?
2.  **Forward Management Guidance & Order Book:** Commentary on **upcoming quarter demand, margin outlook, capex plans, and order inflows** often moves a stock far more than backward-looking quarterly numbers.
3.  **Expectations Already Priced In:** A stock that rallied `25%` into results can sell off on "good" numbers if guidance merely meets expectations, while a beaten-down stock can gap up sharply when results are "less bad" than feared.

---

## 2. Trading Post-Results Price Acceptance (Post-Earnings Drift)

Instead of gambling with oversized positions *before* results drop, many disciplined traders wait for the **post-results reaction**: when a company delivers strong fundamentals and price **gaps up and holds above opening-range support on heavy institutional volume**, multi-week **post-earnings drift** setups often follow cleanly.

---

## 3. Key Takeaways

*   **Operating margins, organic revenue growth, and forward guidance** matter more than one-off headline profit numbers.
*   Watching **post-results price acceptance and volume** avoids binary earnings gamble risk while capturing institutional re-ratings.
