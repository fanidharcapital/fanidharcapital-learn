Spotting **Break of Structure (BOS)** and **Change of Character (CHoCH)** on a single chart can feel abstract until you trace them across two timeframes around an obvious liquidity pool. This case study shows how higher-timeframe trend context and lower-timeframe structural confirmation work together.

## Case Setup: Nifty Pullback Into Prior Swing Low Liquidity

* **Higher Timeframe (Daily):** Nifty 50 is in a broader uptrend making higher highs and higher lows, pulling back toward a prior multi-week swing low where retail stop-losses sit just below support.
* **The Sweep:** On the 15-minute chart, price pokes **35 points below** the prior swing low on a sharp morning flush, triggering sell stops, then immediately prints a strong bullish rejection candle back above support.
* **CHoCH & BOS Sequence:**
  1. **CHoCH (15m):** Price breaks above the most recent lower high formed during the pullback—signalling that immediate downward pressure has shifted.
  2. **Pullback & BOS (15m):** A shallow retest holds above the reclaimed support zone, followed by a clean candle close above the next swing high (**BOS**), confirming bullish continuation in alignment with the daily trend.

## Key Execution Lessons

* **Wait for the Sweep to Reclaim Structure:** Buying the first red candle piercing a swing low risks catching a real breakdown; waiting for reclamation + **CHoCH** filters weak bounces.
* **Anchor Invalidation to Structure:** Placing the stop-loss just beyond the sweep low (sized properly by rupee risk) protects the trade if sellers regain control.
