![Case Study — Initial Balance Breakout vs. Sweep Reversal on Bank Nifty](/images/lessons/lesson-333.png)

Because Bank Nifty carries high intraday beta and strong institutional participation, the **Initial Balance (9:15–10:15 AM IST)** often defines the structural battleground for the rest of the session.

## Scenario 1: Clean IB Breakout With Follow-Through

* **Setup:** Bank Nifty establishes a clear 9:15–10:15 AM range after a supportive domestic cue, with banking heavyweights holding above VWAP.
* **Execution:** Around 10:35 AM, price closes decisively above **IB High**, pulls back lightly without re-entering the IB, and expands higher with rising participation.
* **Risk Control:** Stop-loss sits inside the upper third of the IB rather than chasing extended candles late in the day.

## Scenario 2: IB High Sweep & Reversal

* **Setup:** Bank Nifty creeps up to **IB High** (and prior day high) on slowing momentum.
* **Execution:** A brief spike pokes **90 points above IB High**, triggers breakout buy stops, fails to hold on the next 15-minute close, and slides back below IB High toward mid-IB.
* **Risk Control:** Traders who chased the breakout without acceptance face immediate reversal pressure, while disciplined range traders watch for confirmed re-entry structure.

## Key Execution Lessons

* **Let 10:15 AM Complete Before Judging IB Width:** Early morning spikes before 10:00 AM often form part of the Initial Balance rather than a finished breakout.
* **Never Trade Blindly in the Dead Center of a Wide IB:** Wait for edge boundaries or confirmed breakouts.
