Understanding how Indian equity markets operate beneath the surface is essential for disciplined market education and risk awareness. When an order is placed on **NSE** or **BSE**, execution and settlement involve distinct institutions with clearly defined responsibilities.

## 1. Stock Exchanges vs. Clearing Corporations
* **Stock Exchanges (NSE / BSE):** Operate the electronic matching engine, maintain the central limit order book, enable transparent price discovery, and run market surveillance during trading sessions.
* **Clearing Corporations:** Step in as the central counterparty (CCP) once a trade matches—managing margin requirements, netting obligations across participants, and safeguarding settlement integrity.

## 2. How Price-Time Order Matching Works
* Orders submitted through a broker's Order Management System (OMS) enter the exchange's central limit order book.
* Compatible buy and sell orders match primarily by **price priority** and then **time priority** within each price level.
* Matched trade records then move to the clearing corporation and depositories for post-trade clearing and settlement.

## 3. T+1 Settlement Mechanics in Practice
* Under **T+1 settlement**, trades executed on **Trade Day (`T`)** are settled on the **next working day (`T+1`)**.
* **Funds & Securities Readiness:** Fast settlement cycles make accurate ledger balances, timely margin availability, and clean demat holdings/authorizations critical to smooth post-trade operations.
* **Intraday vs. Delivery:** Intraday positions squared off before market close settle net differences without delivery into demat, whereas delivery trades complete securities and funds transfer on `T+1`.

## 4. Key Takeaways for Market Learners
* Separate **price discovery** (the exchange) from **risk netting and settlement guarantee** (the clearing corporation).
* Treat settlement timelines, margin rules, and liquidity depth as core structural mechanics rather than minor details.
* Focus study on how market plumbing behaves across both calm and high-volatility sessions.
