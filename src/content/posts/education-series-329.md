A **personal trading playbook** is your written operating manual—turning scattered ideas, charts, and lessons into repeatable **Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)**. When markets move quickly, a clear playbook keeps your process steady and verifiable.

## Essential Sections of a Personal Playbook

| Playbook Section | What It Defines |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Pre-Market & Weekly Prep SOP** | Macro/flow check, key levels (PDH/PDL, structure, VWAP), event calendar, and active watchlist |
| **2. Regime & Setup Selection SOP** | How you classify trend vs. range and volatility regime, and which 2–3 core setups you trade |
| **3. Entry, Sizing & Risk SOP** | Exact trigger criteria, stop-loss placement, position-sizing math, and daily/overnight risk caps |
| **4. Adjustment & Exit SOP** | Profit-taking/trailing rules, option adjustment limits (if any), and hard stop / no-revenge-trade rules |
| **5. Post-Session & Weekend Review SOP** | Journaling tags, slippage notes, and weekly attribution review |

## Keeping Your Playbook Alive and Practical

* **Start Lean:** A concise playbook with a few well-understood setups beats a bloated document you never read during market hours.
* **Update Only After Review:** Log proposed rule refinements during weekly/monthly reviews, test them carefully, and keep a clean version history so you always know what rules were active.

## Key Takeaways

* A written playbook turns trading from **ad-hoc reaction into a structured process**.
* Consistency comes from following clear SOPs day after day and refining them deliberately over time.
