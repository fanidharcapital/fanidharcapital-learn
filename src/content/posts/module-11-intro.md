## What You Will Master in Module 11

* **Advanced Market Structure & Liquidity (`Days 301–308`):** Multi-session auction behavior, value areas, liquidity pools around highs/lows, failed breakouts, and structural shifts across timeframes.
* **Volatility Regimes & Term Structure (`Days 309–316`):** Volatility clustering, low-vs.-high volatility transitions, term structure and skew behavior, and adapting setups when regime conditions shift.
* **Multi-Factor & Portfolio Framing (`Days 317–323`):** Combining price structure, flow/volatility context, and risk budgets without overfitting, plus managing overlapping exposures across instruments.
* **Framework Refinement & Review (`Days 324–330`):** Diagnosing edge decay, stress-testing assumptions, refining execution rules, applied multi-concept studies, and the Module 11 Capstone checklist.
