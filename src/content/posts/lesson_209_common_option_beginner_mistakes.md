## 1. Seven Classic Options Mistakes & How to Fix Them

| Beginner Mistake | Why It Hurts | Better Habit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Chasing Cheap Far-OTM "Lottery Tickets"** | Low rupee price hides low probability, weak sensitivity, wide spreads, and frequent `₹0` expiration. | Favor liquid `ATM` / near-`ITM` strikes aligned with real price structure and `1R` sizing. |
| **2. Buying Into High `IV` Before Events & Ignoring IV Crush** | Post-event volatility drop wipes out extrinsic value even if price moves in your direction. | Check `IV` / event expectations; avoid overpaying for inflated event premiums blindly. |
| **3. Ignoring Non-Linear Time Decay (`Theta`)** | Sideways or slow moves near expiry steadily bleed extrinsic value even without hitting a chart stop. | Match holding horizon and `DTE` to expected momentum; don't overstay in slow/choppy tape. |
| **4. Holding `ITM` Stock Options Into Expiry** | Triggers **physical share delivery** obligations, delivery margins, and higher settlement STT. | Close or roll stock options well before expiry-week cutoffs unless taking physical delivery. |
| **5. Using Market Orders on Illiquid Strikes** | Thin order books and wide bid-ask spreads create costly **slippage** on entry and exit. | Trade liquid strikes and use **limit / marketable limit** orders. |
| **6. Sizing Option Sellers Only by Margin Capacity** | Maxing out margin leaves zero buffer for `IV` spikes, gaps, or adverse moves. | Cap trade risk (`1R` / defined stop) and retain **spare cash margin headroom**. |
| **7. Trading Option Chain Without Chart Structure or Stops** | Treating OI walls or PCR as magic predictions without invalidation levels leads to stubborn losses. | Keep **price structure first**, use option chain as context, and honor stops. |

---

## 2. Key Takeaways

*   "Cheap" premiums are not inherently good value—**far-OTM options** have low odds, and **high-`IV` options** before events risk **IV crush**.
*   Always respect **expiry time decay**, **liquidity/spreads**, and **physical settlement rules on stock options**.
