## Overview

Investors accessing Indian capital markets face a foundational structural choice: execute directly through individual stocks and derivatives (DIY), or delegate portfolio construction and rebalancing to pooled vehicles such as Mutual Funds and Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs).

---

## DIY Execution vs. Pooled Vehicles

| Dimension | Direct Equities & F&O | Active Mutual Funds | Index Funds & ETFs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Decision Control** | 100% investor-controlled | Fund manager discretion | Rules-based index replication |
| **Ongoing Cost** | Brokerage, STT, stamp duty, DP charges | Total Expense Ratio (TER: ~0.5%–1.5%) | Ultra-low TER (~0.05%–0.25%) |
| **Tax Efficiency on Rebalance** | Each sale triggers capital gains tax | Internal portfolio churn is tax-exempt | Low turnover; tax triggered only on exit |
| **Time & Skill Demand** | High (research, execution, risk audits) | Low to moderate | Minimal |

---

## Operational & Behavioral Realities

1. **Tax Deferral Inside Pooled Structures:** When a mutual fund manager rebalances holdings internally, unit holders do not incur immediate capital gains tax. In a DIY portfolio, every profitable exit crystallizes tax liability immediately.
2. **Liquidity & Impact Cost in ETFs:** While ETFs trade intraday like shares, screen liquidity can differ from underlying basket liquidity—making limit orders and iNAV checks essential.
3. **The Core-Satellite Hybrid:** Many disciplined market participants hold broad market exposure via low-cost index funds/ETFs (Core) while reserving a defined fraction of capital for direct equities or tactical F&O setups (Satellite).

---

## Key Takeaways

* **Benchmark your DIY alpha:** Direct stock and F&O activity must beat post-cost, post-tax index returns to justify the time and risk expended.
* **Use limit orders on ETFs:** Always verify indicative Net Asset Value (iNAV) before placing orders in thinly traded ETFs.
* **Separate passive compounding from active trading:** Mixing long-term core holdings with short-term tactical trades distorts performance attribution.
