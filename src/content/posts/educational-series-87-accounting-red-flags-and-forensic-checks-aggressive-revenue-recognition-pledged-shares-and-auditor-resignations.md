## 1. Core Forensic Red Flags to Check

Forensic screening looks for structural inconsistencies between reported profits, cash generation, ownership behavior, and oversight quality:

| Forensic Check | What to Look For | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Aggressive Revenue Recognition** | Sharp Q4 sales jumps, extended credit/channel stuffing, booking bill-and-hold or unbilled revenue aggressively, or recognizing uncertain claims early. | Can pull future sales forward or inflate top-line and profit before real cash collections occur. |
| **2. Pledged Promoter Shares** | High or rising percentage of promoter holding pledged as collateral for loans—especially if collateral top-up triggers exist during market dips. | Forced margin calls or lender invocations during sell-offs can trigger heavy supply pressure and governance stress. |
| **3. Auditor Resignations & Qualifications** | Mid-term auditor resignation, frequent auditor switches, qualified/adverse opinions, or recurring emphasis-of-matter notes. | Independent auditors are a key line of defense; abrupt exits or unresolved audit friction warrant immediate caution. |
| **4. Cash Flow & Working-Capital Divergence** | Reported profits rising steadily while `CFO` lags, `Debtor` or `Inventory` days climb sharply, or `Capex` / advances surge in ways that don't match operations. | Helps separate genuine cash-backed compounding from accounting accruals or uncollected balances. |

## 3. Forensic Due Diligence Workflow

1. **Cross-Check `P&L`, `Balance Sheet`, and `Cash Flow Statement` Together:** Never read `Revenue` or `PAT` in isolation—check receivables, unbilled revenue, advances, and `CFO` side by side.
2. **Track Shareholding & Pledge Disclosures Over Time:** Monitor quarterly shareholding patterns for changes in promoter holding, pledge percentages, and invocation/release disclosures.
3. **Read Auditor Resignation & Disclosure Notes Carefully:** Check exchange filings and notes to accounts for stated reasons behind auditor exits, disagreements over valuation/provisioning, or related-party exposures.
4. **Use Red Flags as a Gatekeeper:** A single minor item may have a valid operational explanation, while multiple red flags together should raise your skepticism or keep you on the sidelines.

---

## Key Takeaways

* **Forensic checks focus on verification:** Aggressive revenue recognition, high promoter share pledging, and auditor resignations are critical warning signs to investigate before trusting headline numbers.
* **Pair profit growth with cash-flow and working-capital checks** to see whether earnings are backed by real collections.
* **Strong governance and transparent reporting** are essential pillars alongside valuation and margin of safety.
