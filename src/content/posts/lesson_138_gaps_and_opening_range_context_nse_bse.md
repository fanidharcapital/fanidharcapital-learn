In **Lesson #138**, we focus on one of the most distinctive price-action features of Indian equity markets: **Overnight Gaps and Opening Range Context (NSE / BSE)**. Because NSE and BSE trade from 9:15 AM to 3:30 PM IST—absorbing global overnight cues, RBI/macro announcements, and corporate earnings during the 9:00–9:15 AM pre-open session—how price behaves around an opening gap sets the tone for the entire day.

## 1. The Four Types of Opening Gaps on NSE / BSE

A **Gap** occurs when today's opening price jumps cleanly above yesterday's high (**Gap-Up**) or drops cleanly below yesterday's low (**Gap-Down**), leaving an untraded void on the chart:

| Gap Type | Where It Occurs | Behavioral Meaning & Trading Bias |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Common / Inside-Range Gap** | Opens inside a multi-week sideways box without breaking major structure. | **Low Conviction:** Usually driven by routine overnight noise; high odds of **filling** (retracing back to yesterday's close) within 1–3 sessions. |
| **2. Breakaway Gap** | Gaps cleanly **out of a multi-week base, box, or polarity zone** on heavy volume (often post-earnings or major news). | **High Conviction Initiation:** Urgent institutional repricing. Frequently remains **unfilled** for weeks as price trends away. |
| **3. Runaway (Continuation) Gap** | Occurs midway through an already established strong trend after a brief pullback. | **Momentum Acceleration:** Trend participants are scrambling to add exposure; the gap zone acts as dynamic support/resistance. |
| **4. Exhaustion Gap** | Appears after an extended vertical rally/sell-off on climactic volume, followed by an immediate reversal close. | **Final Climax:** Late retail FOMO chasing the end of a move; once price closes back inside the gap, a sharp trend reversal often follows. |

## 2. Gap-and-Go vs. Gap-Fill: Reading the First 15–60 Minutes

Never blindly short every Gap-Up expecting it to "fill," and never blindly buy every Gap-Up at 9:15 AM. Let the **Opening Range** reveal institutional intent:

*   **The NSE Opening Range Window:** Track the **High and Low of the first 15 minutes (9:15–9:30 AM)** for intraday precision, or the **first 60 minutes (9:15–10:15 AM)** for swing and positional context.
*   **Scenario A — Gap-and-Go (Unfilled Gap Strength):**
    *   Price gaps up above resistance, holds firmly above the **Opening Range Low**, forms a tight flag or Inside Bar during the first hour, and breaks above the **Opening Range High**.
    *   *Action:* Trade with the gap direction; the unfilled gap zone becomes your structural support floor.
*   **Scenario B — Gap-Fill / Trap Reversal:**
    *   Price gaps up into major higher-timeframe resistance, immediately prints a tall selling wick, and breaks decisively **below the Opening Range Low**.
    *   *Action:* Trapped opening buyers liquidate, pulling price down toward **yesterday's closing price** (the Gap Fill magnet).

## 3. Unfilled Gaps as Future Support & Resistance

An **Unfilled Gap** represents a price zone where institutional demand (on a gap-up) or supply (on a gap-down) was so overwhelming that zero shares traded across that strip:

*   When an NSE stock gaps up from `₹740` to `₹765` on strong earnings and trends to `₹830`, a subsequent pullback into the `₹745–₹765` **unfilled gap window** frequently attracts strong institutional buying.
*   If price later closes decisively **all the way through** the gap window, the gap's support thesis is invalidated.

---

## 4. Key Takeaways

*   **Breakaway gaps** out of bases on high volume tend to stay **unfilled** and launch sustained trends; **common gaps** inside ranges tend to **fill quickly**.
*   Use the **NSE Opening Range (9:15–9:30 AM / 9:15–10:15 AM)** to let the market prove whether a gap is holding (**Gap-and-Go**) or failing (**Gap-Fill**).
*   Treat **unfilled gap zones** as active support and resistance shelves on subsequent pullbacks.
