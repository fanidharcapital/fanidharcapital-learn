Every trend pauses to digest gains—your job is to separate a **healthy, buyable trend rest** from **structural decay** that warns a trend is breaking down on NSE/BSE charts.

## 1. Impulse Legs vs. Corrective Pullbacks

In any trending market, price alternates between two distinct phases:

*   **Impulse Leg (With the Trend):** Fast, wide-range candles with strong closes and expanding participation—the market is actively repricing toward a new value level.
*   **Pullback / Retracement (Against the Trend):** A temporary counter-trend pause where early winners book partial profits and institutional buyers reload positions before the next impulse leg.

---

## 2. Diagnostic Checklist: Healthy Pullback vs. Structural Decay

Grade every pullback across **four objective dimensions** before committing capital:

| Dimension | Healthy Trend Pullback | Deep Correction / Trend Decay |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Retracement Depth** | Shallow to moderate: retraces **25% to 50%** of the prior impulse leg and holds cleanly above the prior swing low (`HL`). | Deep: retraces **65% to 85%+** of the prior impulse leg, eroding almost all upward progress and threatening a `CHoCH`. |
| **2. Candle Size & Speed** | **Slow, overlapping, small-bodied candles**—takes more bars to pull back than the impulse leg took to rally. | **Fast, wide-range counter-trend bars** (e.g., two large red Marubozu candles wiping out six days of steady gains). |
| **3. Volume / Delivery Profile** | **Contracting volume** during the pullback—sellers lack aggressive institutional conviction. | **Expanding volume** on down bars—active institutional distribution rather than quiet profit-booking. |
| **4. Structural Destination** | Lands neatly on a **polarity flip zone**, ascending trendline, or rising 20/50-EMA. | Slices straight through horizontal support and dynamic moving averages without pausing. |

---

## 3. Time Corrections vs. Price Corrections

Not all pullbacks move deeply downward. Strong NSE leaders frequently rest through **Time Corrections (High Tight Flags / Sideways Boxes)**:

*   Instead of dropping `8%–10%`, price moves sideways in a tight `2%–3%` band near the highs of the impulse bar for 4–8 sessions.
*   **What It Signals:** Extreme underlying demand. Institutional buyers are absorbing every share offered near the highs without letting the stock dip. When a time correction breaks out above its upper boundary, follow-through is often explosive.

---

## 4. Practical Application on NSE / BSE

1.  **Compare Bar-for-Bar Effort vs. Result:** If an NSE stock rallied `12%` in 3 strong green days and then drifted down `4%` over 6 quiet, low-volume days, buyers remain firmly in control.
2.  **Avoid Catching High-Velocity Counter-Bars:** When a pullback arrives on heavy volume and wide red bodies, step aside—even if it reaches a support level, wait for a multi-bar base or CHoCH before entering.
3.  **Enter When the Pullback Ends, Not While It Is Falling:** Let a bullish trigger candle (Hammer, Bullish Engulfing, or Inside Bar breakout) confirm that the corrective phase has finished and a new impulse leg is starting.

---

## 5. Key Takeaways

*   **Healthy pullbacks** are shallow (`25%–50%`), slow, overlapping, and occur on **contracting volume** into a logical support/polarity zone.
*   **Deep corrections** (`>65%` retracement) driven by wide counter-trend bars and high volume warn of **structural trend decay**.
*   **Time corrections** (tight sideways consolidation near highs) reflect intense institutional demand and often precede strong continuation breakouts.
