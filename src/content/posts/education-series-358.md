Technical analysis works best when **price structure and volume** lead and **indicators** serve as objective measurement tools—never as crowded signals that contradict one another.

## Concept & Scenario Prompts

1. **Trend, Support/Resistance & Price Action:** How do you define swing structure across timeframes, and why do clean zones with clear invalidation beat single hair-thin lines?
2. **Momentum & Volatility Indicators:** What is the difference between a momentum oscillator (such as RSI or MACD) and a volatility/range measure (such as ATR or Bollinger Bands)?
3. **Avoiding Indicator Overload:** Why do stacking five or six overlapping indicators on one chart often create analysis paralysis instead of better entries?

## Quick Self-Check

* Can you explain **why** a setup has an edge in plain price-and-volume terms without hiding behind indicator jargon?
* Do you place stop-losses at **structural invalidation levels** rather than arbitrary round numbers?
