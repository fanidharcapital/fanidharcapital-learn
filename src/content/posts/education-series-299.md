In fast-moving macro sessions, strong analysis can still lose money if execution is sloppy. Wide bid-ask spreads, market-order slippage, and impulsive entries during headline spikes are completely avoidable with a standardized pre-event and post-event checklist.

## Phase 1: Pre-Event Checklist (T–24 Hours to 30 Minutes Before Release)

* **Verify Event Calendar:** Confirm exact release timestamp (e.g., 10:00 AM IST for RBI MPC, 11:00 AM IST for Union Budget, 5:30 PM / 6:00 PM IST for CPI).
* **Audit Open Exposure:** Convert any unhedged overnight futures or naked short options into defined-risk spreads if holding across the announcement.
* **Pre-Set Reduced Lot Size:** Reduce standard trade quantity by **40%–60%** to account for wider post-event ATR and stop distances.
* **Identify Key Reference Levels:** Mark previous day high/low, weekly pivot, and major option open-interest strikes on your chart before the headline drops.

## Phase 2: Announcement Window (T–0 to T+15 Minutes)

* **Hands Off Market Orders:** Never fire market orders in illiquid options during the first 60–120 seconds of a policy or CPI release; use limit orders with defined price buffers.
* **Let the First Spike Print:** Observe whether the initial move holds or immediately reverses back inside the pre-event range.
* **Check Bond Yields & Currency Confirmation:** Confirm that G-Sec yields or USD/INR align with the index move rather than chasing an isolated equity head-fake.

## Phase 3: Post-Event Execution & Review (T+15 Minutes Onward)

* **Enter Only on Structure Confirmation:** Require a 15-minute candle close and a clean retest of VWAP or the post-event range boundary.
* **Enforce Hard Daily Loss Limit:** If two post-event setups fail, shut down trading for the session—choppy post-event days punish revenge trading.
* **Log Slippage & Spread Quality:** Record actual fill vs. intended price in your trade journal to refine future event execution.
