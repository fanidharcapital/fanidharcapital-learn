![Case Study — VWAP & Standard Deviation Bands on a Trend Day vs. Range Day](/images/lessons/lesson-335.png)

**VWAP** and its **±1 / ±2 Standard Deviation (SD) bands** behave very differently depending on whether the session is trending or balanced. Misreading a trend day as overextended—or treating a choppy range day as a breakout—is a classic intraday mistake.

## Trend Day Behaviour Around VWAP & SD Bands

* VWAP slopes steadily upward (or downward), and price holds on one side of VWAP after morning structure forms.
* Pullbacks tag VWAP or the **±1 SD** band and bounce cleanly in trend direction; even **±2 SD** can be walked along during strong institutional sessions.

## Range Day Behaviour Around VWAP & SD Bands

* VWAP stays relatively flat as price oscillates back and forth across it.
* Pushes toward **+2 SD** or **-2 SD** often stall and revert toward VWAP rather than extending into a one-way trend.

## Key Execution Lessons

* **Check VWAP Slope and Swing Structure Together:** Never fade a strong trend solely because price touches +2 SD while VWAP is rising sharply with higher lows.
* **Respect Choppy Crossings:** Frequent two-way crosses of a flat VWAP signal range discipline and smaller expectations.
