Sound trading in Indian markets requires knowing the rules of the road—how exchanges curb speculative excess, how large trades and disclosures are reported, and how investor protection mechanisms operate.

## Concept & Scenario Prompts

1. **Surveillance & Price Bands:** Why do exchanges apply price bands, trade-to-trade settlement, or ASM / GSM / ESM frameworks on certain stocks, and how does that affect liquidity and position sizing?
2. **Bulk Deals, Block Deals & Disclosures:** What distinguishes a bulk deal from a block deal, and why do insider-trading, takeover, and promoter-pledge disclosures matter when researching a stock?
3. **Grievance Redressal & Safeguards:** Where do investors turn for formal dispute resolution and arbitration when a broker issue cannot be resolved directly?

## Quick Self-Check

* Before trading an unfamiliar small-cap or mid-cap stock, do you check **price bands, trade-to-trade status, and surveillance alerts**?
* Do you understand how **2FA, authorized device controls, and client fund safeguards** protect your trading account?
