Whether you trade short-term swings or hold medium-term positions, understanding **business quality, cash generation, and valuation context** keeps you from overpaying for fragile stories.

## Concept & Scenario Prompts

1. **Business Model & Economic Moats:** What gives a company durable pricing power or cost advantage—and what competitive shifts can erode a moat over time?
2. **Statements & Cash Flow Quality:** Why can a company report rising accounting profit while operating cash flow or free cash flow lags—and what red flags show up on balance sheets and debt schedules?
3. **Valuation Context:** Why should valuation ratios (such as P/E, EV/EBITDA, or P/B) always be read alongside **growth durability, return ratios (ROE/ROCE), and interest-rate/cycle context** rather than as standalone numbers?

## Quick Self-Check

* Can you separate a **cyclical earnings peak** (where a low P/E can be a value trap) from **compounding earnings growth**?
* Do you check **balance-sheet leverage, working capital, and cash conversion** before taking multi-week or multi-month exposure?
