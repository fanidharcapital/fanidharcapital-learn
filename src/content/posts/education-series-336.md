![Case Study — Breakaway Gap vs. Gap-Fill Session at the Opening Bell](/images/lessons/lesson-336.png)

A morning gap at 9:15 AM IST is just the opening snapshot; how price behaves during the first 30–60 minutes determines whether the gap **fills** or **breaks away**.

## Case 1: Gap Inside Range Fading to Fill

* **Setup:** A stock gaps up **2.2%** after mild news, but lands squarely beneath a well-established resistance shelf inside a multi-week range.
* **Execution:** Early buyers fail to hold above the opening 15-minute high; sellers press price back through the opening level and close the gap later in the session.

## Case 2: Breakaway Gap Clearing Multi-Week Resistance

* **Setup:** A stock coils below a major resistance level for three weeks, then gaps up **3.5%** on strong earnings/catalysts right above the breakout ceiling.
* **Execution:** Buyers defend the opening range low (staying well clear of the prior range top), volume stays elevated, and the gap remains unfilled as a multi-day trend begins.

## Key Execution Lessons

* **Location Dictates Probability:** Gaps *into* major overhead resistance often face supply; gaps *out of* clean multi-week bases carry stronger breakaway potential.
* **Avoid Blindly Fading Every Gap:** Always let early intraday structure confirm rejection before trading a gap fill.
