A trade journal becomes truly valuable the moment you **tag every trade** and review patterns across categories rather than staring only at daily P&L.

## Case Walkthrough: Reviewing 40 Tagged Trades

* **Setup Breakdown:** Core trend-pullback and defined-risk spread setups in aligned regimes show steady positive expectancy.
* **Error Tag Discovery:** A cluster of losses shares two tags—`chasing-late-entry` and `revenge-trade-after-stop`—accounting for a disproportionate share of total drawdown.
* **Targeted Fix:** Adding a mandatory 10-minute cool-off after any two consecutive stops and refusing entries extended past structure immediately cleans up those leaks without changing core setups.

## Key Execution Lessons

* **Tag Rule Errors Explicitly:** Naming the exact mistake (`FOMO`, `oversized`, `moved-stop`, `no-stop`) makes recurring habits impossible to ignore.
* **Fix High-Cost Leaks First:** Eliminating self-inflicted errors often delivers the fastest improvement in net consistency.
