Welcome to **Module 08: Greeks & Strategies**. This introductory page gives you a clear summary of what **Days 211–240** cover across option sensitivities and multi-leg strategy design.

---

## What You Will Master in Module 08

* **Option Greeks & Sensitivities (`Days 211–218`):** How **Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega, and Rho** behave across strikes and expiries, plus reading net position exposure rather than isolated legs.
* **Directional & Volatility Strategies (`Days 219–227`):** Bull/bear debit and credit spreads, ratio/backspread concepts, long/short straddles and strangles, and matching structure to trend and volatility regimes.
* **Range, Calendar & Hedging Setups (`Days 228–233`):** Iron condors, iron butterflies, calendar and diagonal spreads, hedging equity or futures exposure, and payoff trade-offs.
* **Adjustments, Execution & Review (`Days 234–240`):** Profit targets, stop rules, rolling legs, multi-leg execution and slippage, position sizing by max loss, case studies, and the Module 08 Capstone checklist.
