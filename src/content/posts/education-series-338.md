Quiet, narrow-range days after a strong trend leg often look boring—yet **volatility coils** frequently store energy before the next directional leg.

## From Coil to Expansion

* **Compression Phase:** Over five sessions, daily ranges shrink, ATR trends downward, and price forms converging highs and lows in a tight coil while option IV cools.
* **Expansion Trigger:** Price closes decisively above the coil's upper boundary with expanding candle range and volume, followed by higher lows on subsequent sessions.
* **Execution Adjustment:** Once expansion begins, size positions to match the wider ATR and trail stops structurally rather than using tight pre-coil stops that get clipped by normal expansion noise.

## Key Execution Lessons

* **Stay Patient Inside the Coil:** Overtrading tiny inside-range swings during compression wastes focus and transaction costs.
* **Let Price Pick the Direction:** Never assume a coil must break in your preferred direction until price exits and holds.
