Welcome to **Module 12: Case Studies**. This introductory page gives you a clear summary of what **Days 331–365** cover as you apply the full roadmap through practical examples and reviews.

---

## What You Will Master in Module 12

* **Foundations, Analysis & Structure Cases (`Days 331–339`):** End-to-end reviews applying market mechanics, business fundamentals, technical indicators, and price-action structure to real market scenarios.
* **Derivatives, Hedging & Macro Cases (`Days 340–348`):** Case studies covering futures positioning, option payoff and Greeks behavior, event/earnings reactions, and institutional flow context.
* **Risk, Mistakes & Decision Audits (`Days 349–357`):** Reviewing setups that worked and setups that failed, sizing and drawdown audits, emotional slip-ups, and structured self-review exercises.
* **365-Day Synthesis & Capstone (`Days 358–365`):** Consolidating your personal ruleset, long-term study and review habits, and completing the final 365-day roadmap wrap-up.
