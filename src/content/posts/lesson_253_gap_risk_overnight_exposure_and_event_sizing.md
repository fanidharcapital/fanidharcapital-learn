---

## 1. Why Stop-Loss Orders Cannot Prevent Overnight Gaps

*   Between NSE's `3:30 PM` close and `9:15 AM` open, global markets, macro data, corporate announcements, and geopolitical headlines keep moving.
*   If you hold overnight cash stocks, futures, or naked short options and the market opens **`3%–8%` against you**, your stop-loss triggers at the **opening gap price**—turning a planned `1R` loss into a `2R–4R` gap loss.

---

## 2. Event Calendar Awareness & Pre-Event Risk Controls

Always check the **corporate and macro event calendar** before carrying overnight risk:
*   **Stock-Specific Catalysts:** Quarterly earnings dates, board meetings, bonus/dividend/M&A announcements, and regulatory rulings.
*   **Index / Macro Catalysts:** **RBI Monetary Policy (`MPC`)** announcements, **Union Budget**, election outcomes, and major US Fed / inflation releases.

**Three Clean Ways to Control Event & Gap Risk:**
1.  **Reduce Overnight Position Size:** Cut overnight lot or share quantity by `50%` (or more) so even a `2x–3x` gap stays within acceptable capital tolerance.
2.  **Switch to Defined-Risk Option Spreads:** Replace naked futures or naked short options with **vertical spreads, Iron Condors, or Protective Puts/Collars** where worst-case gap loss is mathematically capped at the wing strike.
3.  **Go Flat Ahead of Binary Surprises:** If your edge is technical price action rather than event gambling, closing or trimming before a major binary release is often the highest-discipline play.

---

## 3. Key Takeaways

*   Stop-loss orders fill at the **next available market price**—they do not protect against overnight or event gaps.
*   Check the **earnings and macro calendar** daily, and use **smaller overnight size or defined-risk option spreads** across major catalysts.
