Stop-loss orders only protect you while the exchange is open and liquid. When an unexpected geopolitical shock, weekend policy announcement, or global overnight sell-off hits, Nifty and Bank Nifty can open 2%–5% away from the previous close—skipping right past resting stop orders. A **macro risk budget** ensures no single overnight gap can threaten your trading capital.

## Four Pillars of Overnight & Macro Risk Control

### 1. Define a Maximum Overnight Gap Budget
* Calculate the worst-case loss across all open positions if the index gaps **3% against you** at 9:15 AM IST.
* Keep that worst-case overnight gap loss within **2%–3% of total trading capital**. If the stress test exceeds your limit, trim exposure before the 3:30 PM close.

### 2. Replace Naked Overnight Positions With Defined-Risk Spreads
* Convert naked short options or unhedged futures into **vertical spreads or iron condors** before carrying positions across macro events or weekends.
* Buying low-cost far-OTM protective wings caps tail risk and stabilizes margin requirements during volatility spikes.

### 3. Cap Correlated Sector Concentration
* Holding long positions in four different banking or NBFC stocks is effectively one leveraged bet on RBI rate expectations. Cap total exposure per macro theme or sector.

### 4. Pre-Planned Morning Gap Protocol (9:00–9:30 AM IST)
* Check pre-open equilibrium price between 9:00 AM and 9:08 AM IST.
* Avoid panic-dumping into illiquid opening seconds unless your pre-defined worst-case risk threshold is breached; wait for the first 5-to-15-minute opening range to establish liquidity before adjusting hedges.

## Summary Checklist

* Stress-test every overnight book against a **3% adverse gap** before market close.
* Keep **event-week risk budgets smaller** than calm-trend weeks.
* Treat protective wings and position caps as non-negotiable operating costs of professional trading.
