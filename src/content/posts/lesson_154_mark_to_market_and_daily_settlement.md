In **Lesson #153**, we covered contract specifications and notional value. In **Lesson #154**, we look at how futures P&L is settled every single trading day: **Mark-to-Market (MTM)**. Unlike holding a cash stock where unrealized gains or losses sit quietly on screen until you sell, futures positions are revalued and settled daily against the official settlement price.

![Educational Series #154: Mark-to-Market (MTM) & Daily Settlement](/images/lessons/lesson-154.png)

---

## 1. How Mark-to-Market (MTM) Works

At the end of each trading day while a futures position remains open:

1.  **Daily Revaluation:** The clearing corporation marks open futures positions to the daily settlement price.
2.  **Loss Debit / Gain Credit:** MTM losses reduce your available margin balance, while MTM profits are credited per clearing/broker settlement rules.
3.  **Reset of Reference Base:** Each settled day establishes a fresh daily reference price for the next session's MTM calculation.

---

## 2. Why MTM Can Trigger Margin Shortfalls

Even if your ultimate target is still intact on the chart, **adverse MTM swings** can create a **margin shortfall** if your account has zero cash buffer:

*   **Example:** You enter a long futures position and block nearly 100% of your free cash for initial margin. On Day 1, price drops against you before your stop-loss or before bouncing.
*   **Consequence:** Your available margin drops below required levels. If you do not replenish funds on time, **margin shortfall penalties** can apply and your broker's risk team may square off positions to protect against default.

---

## 3. Practical MTM Discipline

*   **Keep an Idle Cash Buffer:** Never deploy 100% of your available margin into open futures trades. Maintain unallocated cash buffer to absorb adverse MTM fluctuations without scrambling for funds.
*   **Monitor Overnight & Multi-Day Holds:** Carrying a futures position across sessions means exposing yourself to overnight/opening MTM shifts at the next market open.

---

## 4. Key Takeaways

*   Futures positions undergo **daily Mark-to-Market (MTM)** settlement while open.
*   MTM losses eat into your margin cushion and can trigger shortfall penalties or forced square-offs if unbuffered.
*   Always hold a **cash buffer** above your required upfront margin.
