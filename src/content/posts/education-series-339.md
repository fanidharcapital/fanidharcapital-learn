Watching **Nifty 50**, **Bank Nifty**, **India VIX**, and **Gift Nifty** together helps you see whether a move has broad cross-market backing or hidden friction underneath.

## Case Setup: Headline Green Tick With Cross-Asset Warning Signs

* **Morning Cue:** Gift Nifty points to a mild positive open after a steady overnight session, and Nifty 50 ticks slightly higher mid-morning on a couple of large-cap movers.
* **Divergence Clues:** Bank Nifty fails to reclaim its morning high and slips below VWAP, while **India VIX** rises instead of cooling—signalling hedging demand beneath a narrow surface.
* **Afternoon Outcome:** Without banking confirmation, Nifty's morning push stalls near resistance and rotates sideways into the close.

## Key Execution Lessons

* **Use Cross-Asset Divergence to Calibrate Conviction:** When Bank Nifty lags and VIX rises during a tepid index bounce, keep position sizes modest and avoid chasing extended calls.
* **Anchor Decisions to Chart Structure & Risk:** Never treat an external ticker as a standalone buy/sell signal without a valid setup and stop.
