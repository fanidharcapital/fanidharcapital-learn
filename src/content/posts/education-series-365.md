Reaching **Lesson #365** completes the full **12-week educational series** for **Shree Fanidhar Capital**. Finishing the curriculum is a major milestone—and the real payoff comes from turning these frameworks into calm, repeatable daily habits over the months ahead.

## Your Post-Curriculum Roadmap

1. **Take the Final Assessment (Module 13 / Final Exam):** Test your recall and applied judgment across market mechanics, fundamentals, technicals, derivatives, volatility, and risk management to identify any weaker spots for targeted review.
2. **Maintain Your Living Playbook & SOPs:** Keep your core setups, volatility filters, position-sizing formulas, and *no-trade* rules written down—and refine them only through structured weekly/monthly reviews.
3. **Audit Every Trade With Consistent Tags:** Log setup type, market regime, option/position structure, and rule adherence on every trade so your journal continuously points you toward high-expectancy habits and away from costly leaks.
4. **Prioritize Capital Preservation & Patience:** Market cycles will always bring quiet ranges, sharp trends, surprises, and losing streaks. Protecting downside risk and waiting for high-clarity setups keeps you compounding sustainably across every cycle.

## Final Capstone Checklist

* Do you trade **planned setups with defined risk** rather than reacting to headlines or FOMO?
* Do you match **position size and option structure** to volatility and market structure?
* Do you treat **every loss within risk rules as data** and every rule violation as a process item to fix?

Thank you for following the complete **365-lesson, 12-week journey**—keep learning, keep journaling, and trade with discipline.
