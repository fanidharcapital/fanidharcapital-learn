## 1. Core Pillars of Indian Trader Record-Keeping Hygiene

1.  **Complete Broker & Exchange Statements:** Download and archive annual/monthly **Tax P&L statements, contract notes, ledger summaries, STT/stamp-duty proofs, and demat/bank statements** across every broker and entity you use—never rely on memory or partial screenshots at filing time.
2.  **Understanding Income Classification, Turnover & Audit Rules:** In India, trading income classification (such as business income vs. capital gains depending on instrument and activity), **F&O turnover calculation**, **tax audit applicability**, and **allowable business expenses** follow specific statutory rules and thresholds that are periodically updated. Maintain accurate books and confirm current rules and thresholds with your `CA`.
3.  **Clean Separation & Expense Hygiene:** Keep trading bank/demat records organized and separate from personal household spending, and retain clear invoices/proofs for legitimate trading-related expenses (such as data/charting tools, advisory/educational resources, internet/hardware, and professional fees) as advised by your `CA`.

---

## 2. Why Year-Round Hygiene Beats Year-End Scramble

Exporting statements monthly and keeping a clean ledger all year turns tax season into a **structured verification** rather than a stressful hunt for missing files—and lets you track **after-tax, after-cost net performance** accurately.

---

## 3. Key Takeaways

*   Archive **Tax P&L reports, contract notes, ledgers, and expense proofs** systematically throughout the year rather than scrambling at filing time.
*   Verify **income classification, turnover/audit thresholds, and deductible expenses** under current Indian tax law with a qualified **Chartered Accountant**.
