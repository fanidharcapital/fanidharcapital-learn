In **Lesson #264**, we prepared levels, volatility/event context, and `1R` sizes ahead of the open. In **Lesson #265**, we focus on **Live-Market Execution & Alert Workflow**—using alerts, conditional/limit orders, and disciplined order gates so you don't stare at screens waiting to click on impulse.

![Educational Series #265: Live-Market Execution & Alert Workflow](/images/lessons/lesson-265.png)

---

## 1. Let Alerts Bring Price to Your Plan

*   Instead of watching every candle tick-by-tick and tempting yourself into low-conviction entries, place **price or structure alerts** at your pre-planned breakout, pullback, or support/resistance levels.
*   When an alert triggers, step back to your **playbook checklist**: confirm structure, `VIX`/`IV` fit, liquidity, and `1R` risk before placing the order.

---

## 2. Using Conditional & Limit Orders Cleanly

*   **Conditional / Limit Orders Over Chasing:** Use limit prices, basket/spread orders (for multi-leg option trades), or conditional triggers with pre-defined **stop-loss and profit/invalidation levels** rather than chasing extended candles with rushed market orders.
*   **Respect Circuit Breakers & Step-Away Windows:** Once a setup is entered with its stop and target in place, let the trade play out according to plan—and if your **daily loss limit** or a **rule-break circuit breaker** triggers, stop entering new orders immediately.

---

## 3. Key Takeaways

*   Use **chart alerts** and **playbook verification** so price comes to your levels rather than pulling you into FOMO chases.
*   Pair **limit/spread/conditional orders** with pre-set stops and circuit breakers to keep live execution calm and repeatable.
