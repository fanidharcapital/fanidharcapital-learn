## 1. A Focused Pre-Market Checklist

Keep pre-market prep structured and time-boxed—enough to see the battlefield clearly without getting overwhelmed by headlines:

*   **Global Cues & `Gift Nifty`:** Check overnight US/Asian index direction, crude/currency moves, and **`Gift Nifty` indication** to gauge likely opening gap context—without treating an early print as a guaranteed all-day trend.
*   **Volatility & Event Calendar (`India VIX` & Catalysts):** Note current **`India VIX` / option `IV` tone** and check whether today or tonight brings scheduled **quarterly earnings, RBI policy/macro releases, or expiry sessions** that warrant smaller sizing or defined-risk structures.
*   **Key Structure Levels & Watchlist:** Mark prior session highs/lows, major swing support/resistance zones, and invalidation levels on a tight watchlist (`2–4` liquid indices or stocks), and pre-calculate **`1R` lot/share sizes** before the market opens.

---

## 2. Avoid Morning Bell FOMO

When your levels, event risks, and `1R` sizes are already mapped out before `9:15 AM`, you don't need to chase the first chaotic opening candle out of excitement or fear of missing out. You simply wait for your levels and triggers to develop.

---

## 3. Key Takeaways

*   Review **global cues, `Gift Nifty`, `India VIX`, and the event calendar** alongside **key chart levels** before market open.
*   Pre-calculating **`1R` sizes** and a focused watchlist keeps your `9:15 AM` open calm and structured.
