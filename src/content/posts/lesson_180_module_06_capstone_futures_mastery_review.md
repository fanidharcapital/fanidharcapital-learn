## 1. Complete Pre-Trade Futures Checklist

Run every potential futures trade through these **8 non-negotiable gates**:

1.  **Layer 1 — Price Structure:** Is there a clear trend/range context, structural trigger, and exact invalidation level from Modules 04–05?
2.  **Layer 2 — Open Interest & Volume:** Does OI + volume confirm genuine participation (Long/Short Buildup) rather than a weak unwinding/short-covering trap?
3.  **Layer 3 — Basis & Carry Check:** Is basis within normal carry, or distorted by dividends, earnings, or expiry-week rollovers?
4.  **Contract & Liquidity Check:** Have you verified current lot size, tick value, bid-ask spread, and order-book depth in the active contract month?
5.  **Operational & Calendar Gate:** Is the stock clear of **F&O ban (MWPL)**, imminent **earnings/major events**, and **expiry-week physical delivery** cutoffs?
6.  **`1R` Lot Sizing Calculation:** Did you compute $\text{Lots} = \lfloor 1R \div (\text{Stop Distance} \times \text{Lot Size}) \rfloor$ and round **down**?
7.  **Margin & Cash Buffer Check:** After blocking SPAN + exposure margin, do you still retain ample **free cash buffer** for daily MTM swings and peak margin checks?
8.  **Execution & Circuit Breaker Plan:** Are your entry order type (`Limit` / marketable limit), stop-loss (`SL-L` with buffer), target plan, and **daily loss circuit breaker (`2R–3R`)** defined before clicking Buy/Sell?

## 2. Module 06 Mastery Verification

Before moving to **Module 07**, verify that you can answer all five capstone questions without hesitation:

1.  **Settlement Distinction:** Why are **index futures** cash-settled while **stock futures** on NSE are physically settled—and what happens if you ignore expiry-week rules on a stock future?
2.  **Margin vs. Risk:** If a futures contract requires `₹1,80,000` margin on a `₹9,00,000` notional value, why is `₹1,80,000` **not** your maximum risk?
3.  **OI Interpretation:** How do you differentiate **Long Buildup** from **Short Covering** when both show rising prices?
4.  **Pricing & Carry:** What three variables govern theoretical **cost of carry**, and why must **basis** converge toward zero at expiry?
5.  **Overnight & Ban Controls:** Why can an overnight gap at `9:15 AM IST` slip past a stop-loss, and what happens to your trading permissions if a stock crosses `95%` of **MWPL**?
