A portfolio can look diversified on paper—holding six different tickers—while actually carrying one concentrated macro or directional bet. Understanding **correlation** and **hidden leverage** across cash equities and F&O prevents accidental overexposure.

## Where Hidden Risk Hides

* **Correlated Sector & Theme Bets:** Long positions in multiple banks, NBFCs, or rate-sensitive stocks often move together when yields or RBI liquidity expectations shift—multiplying your exposure to a single catalyst.
* **Effective Leverage in F&O:** Margin requirements in futures and options allow large notional exposure relative to cash collateral. Even defined-risk spreads and short options carry **delta, gamma, and vega exposure** that can expand rapidly during sharp moves.
* **Cash + F&O Overlap:** Holding long cash stocks in a sector while simultaneously selling puts or buying calls on the same sector stacks directional risk on the exact same theme.

## Practical Portfolio-Level Checks

1. **Count Correlated Risk as One Theme:** Cap total risk across correlated positions rather than treating each ticker as independent.
2. **Stress-Test Combined Exposure:** Check what happens to your entire open book—cash plus F&O—if the index drops 2%–3% or VIX jumps sharply.

## Key Takeaways

* Diversification requires **genuinely different risk drivers**, not just multiple tickers moving in lockstep.
* Always evaluate **total combined exposure** across cash and derivatives rather than looking at each trade in isolation.
