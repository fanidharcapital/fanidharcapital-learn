In **Lesson #265**, we structured live-market alerts and order execution. In **Lesson #266**, we close the trading day with a **Post-Market Debrief & End-of-Day (`EOD`) Audit**—capturing clean lessons while memory is fresh, checking account safety, and closing screens cleanly.

![Educational Series #266: Post-Market Debrief & EOD Audit](/images/lessons/lesson-266.png)

---

## 1. A Crisp Three-Part End-of-Day Audit

1.  **Log & Grade Discipline While Fresh:** Record every trade's entry/exit, planned `1R`, realized `R`-multiple, `MFE`/`MAE`, friction/slippage, **rule-adherence score**, and emotional tag—alongside one honest note on what you did well or where you drifted.
2.  **Verify Margin Health & Overnight Exposure:** Check realized + open P&L against your **daily loss limit**, review remaining margin buffer, and verify any overnight equity, futures, or option positions for **gap/event risk** and NSE/BSE settlement/expiry rules.
3.  **Draft Tomorrow's Watchlist & Close Screens:** Jot down key levels and potential setups for the next session, then **close your trading terminal** so your mind switches off and recovers.

---

## 2. Consistency Compounds Quietly

A disciplined `15–20` minute `EOD` audit every day prevents small execution slips from becoming ingrained habits and ensures you enter the next session with a clean slate and clear priorities.

---

## 3. Key Takeaways

*   Log trades, `R`-multiples, and **rule-adherence grades** daily while details are fresh—never rely on hazy weekend memory.
*   Check **daily limit status, margin health, and overnight/event exposure**, then close terminals cleanly to set up tomorrow's watchlist.
