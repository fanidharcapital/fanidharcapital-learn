A basic P&L log only tells you *how much* you made or lost—not *why*. Structured **trade journaling and performance attribution** breaks your results down by setup, market regime, and execution discipline so you can double down on strengths and fix recurring leaks.

## High-Value Tags to Log on Every Trade

* **Setup / Playbook Tag:** Pullback continuation, breakout, range fade, credit spread, iron condor, calendar spread, or event setup.
* **Regime & Volatility Tag:** Trending vs. range-bound, low VIX vs. elevated VIX/IVR, expiry day vs. normal day.
* **Rule-Adherence Tag:** *Followed Plan* vs. *Rule Error* (such as FOMO entry, early exit, moved stop, oversized lot, or revenge trade).
* **Execution Quality Note:** Actual fill vs. planned level and any slippage around fast moves.

## Turning Journal Data Into Action

1. **Separate "Rule-Error Losses" From "Valid Setup Losses":** Losing on a valid setup within risk limits is part of trading; losing because you broke a rule is a fixable leak.
2. **Review Weekly & Monthly Buckets:** Look for setups that consistently produce positive expectancy and trim or pause low-edge habits that drag down net results.

## Key Takeaways

* Tagging **setups, regimes, and rule adherence** turns raw trade history into actionable feedback.
* Small improvements in eliminating **rule-error trades** often boost net performance faster than searching for a "secret" indicator.
