In **Lessons #140–#143**, we synthesized context, regime, volume, and multi-timeframe analysis. Now, in **Lesson #144**, we put this synthesis into action by defining the specific, repeatable **High-Probability Price-Action Setups** that form the foundation of a successful trading playbook for Indian equities (NSE/BSE). These setups share one common trait: They all occur at validated structural levels, offering positive Expectancy and Asymmetric Risk/Reward.

## 1. Setup #1: The Trend Pullback to Structure (Continuation)

This setup aligns you with the dominant order flow during a confirmed Trend Regime (Lesson #141).

*   **The Context:** Established Uptrend (`HH+HL`) or Downtrend (`LH+LL`).
*   **The Event:** Price corrects (pulls back) against the trend.
*   **The Target Entry Area (Confluence Zone):**
    *   **Static:** Major horizontal Support/Resistance (Polarity Flip) Zone (Lesson #133).
    *   **Dynamic:** The rising 20-EMA or 50-EMA (which acts as a moving trendline).
*   **The Trigger (M15/H1):** A bullish rejection candle (Hammer, Bullish Engulfing) or an Inside Bar breakout **right at the confluence zone**.
*   **The Stop-Loss:** Just below the swing low of the pullback (for longs).

---

## 2. Setup #2: The Range Extreme Reversal (Mean Reversion)

This setup targets the high probability that price will rotate within a well-defined Choppy/Range Regime (Lesson #141).

*   **The Context:** Established horizontal Box Consolidation (Lesson #137) with at least two touches top and bottom.
*   **The Event:** Price reaches the extreme of the range.
*   **The Target Entry Area:** The **Supply Edge (Upper 20%)** or **Demand Edge (Lower 20%)** of the box.
*   **The Trigger (M15/H1):** A clear bearish rejection candle (Shooting Star, Bearish Engulfing) at Resistance, or a bullish rejection candle (Hammer, Buying Tail) at Support.
*   **The Stop-Loss:** Just beyond the extreme wick tip of the rejection candle.
*   **The Profit Target:** The opposite side of the range, or the Mid-Range (for conservative targets).

---

## 3. Setup #3: The Breakout–Retest (Continuation / Regime Shift)

This setup trades the moment a level changes its structural role (Polarity Flip, Lesson #133).

*   **The Context:** Price is breaking out of a long period of Compression (Accumulation/Distribution, Lesson #139) or a major horizontal resistance/support level.
*   **The Event (Phase 1 - Ignition):** Price closes decisively beyond the level on high volume (Lesson #142).
*   **The Event (Phase 2 - Retest):** Price pulls back to the **broken level** (now flipped in role) on contracting volume.
*   **The Trigger (M15/H1):** A rejection candle (of either color) that finds support exactly at the flipped level.
*   **The Stop-Loss:** Just below the flipped support level (for longs).
*   **The Profit Target:** At least `2×` the risk, or the next higher Daily/Weekly structural level.

---

## 4. Key Takeaways

*   High-probability setups always occur at **validated structural levels**. Never trade "in the middle."
*   **Trend Pullbacks** align you with dominant flow. **Range Reversals** trade mean reversion in balance markets. **Breakout-Retests** trade structural role reversals.
*   Every entry must be confirmed by a **price-action trigger** (a specific candle footprint) and a **volume signature** (where applicable).
