## The Five-Step Institutional & Macro Decision Funnel

1. **Macro & Policy Backdrop (Top Layer):**
   * Assess inflation trajectory, RBI rate/liquidity stance, global yields, Brent crude, and USD/INR.
   * *Question:* Are macro forces expanding or tightening system liquidity?
2. **Institutional Flow & Derivatives Positioning:**
   * Track multi-day FII vs. DII cash trends, participant-wise index futures positioning, and rollover sentiment.
   * *Question:* Are large institutions accumulating on dips, hedging downside tail risk, or distributing into strength?
3. **Market Breadth & Volatility Regime:**
   * Check India VIX, NSE Advance-Decline trends, and the percentage of Nifty 500 stocks above their 50-DMA.
   * *Question:* Is participation broad and supportive of breakouts, or narrow and defensive?
4. **Sector Relative Strength Selection:**
   * Rank sector indices against Nifty 50 to isolate institutional leaders and avoid structural laggards.
   * *Question:* Which 2 sectors are attracting persistent relative inflows right now?
5. **Chart Structure, Strategy & Risk Budget (Execution Layer):**
   * Align entry triggers with support/resistance, pick the right equity or options spread structure for current IV skew/term structure, and size positions within your macro risk budget.
   * *Question:* Where is my exact price invalidation, and is my worst-case overnight gap loss capped?

## Module 10 Mastery Self-Check

* Can you explain how a 10-year G-Sec yield move or Brent crude spike transmits into specific NSE sectors?
* Do you check **India VIX and front-week vs. next-week IV** before choosing between debit spreads, credit spreads, or calendars around events?
* Are you sizing down and waiting for post-headline structure around RBI policy, Budget, and CPI releases?
