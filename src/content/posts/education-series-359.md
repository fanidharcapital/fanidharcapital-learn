Futures offer linear exposure, transparent pricing, and clean hedging mechanics—paired with **mark-to-market cash flows, leverage, and expiry roll dynamics**.

## Concept & Scenario Prompts

1. **Spot vs. Futures & Basis:** What drives the spread (**basis**) between a futures contract and the underlying spot price, and why does basis converge toward zero at expiration?
2. **Mark-to-Market & Margin:** How does daily mark-to-market settlement work, and why can a modest adverse move trigger margin pressure if leverage is too high?
3. **Rollovers & Hedging:** When and how do traders roll futures from near month to next month, and how can index futures hedge a correlated equity portfolio during uncertain stretches?

## Quick Self-Check

* Do you track **open interest and rollover activity** into monthly expiry rather than getting caught by thin liquidity or physical settlement rules on stock futures?
* Can you calculate **notional exposure** so you know the true leverage behind your futures position?
