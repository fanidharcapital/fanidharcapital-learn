Reading an **option chain** requires connecting strike-level **Open Interest (OI)** and **OI change** with live spot price action—never treating open interest numbers in isolation.

## Concept & Scenario Prompts

1. **Intrinsic vs. Extrinsic Value:** How do you separate an option's intrinsic value from its time/extrinsic value across ITM, ATM, and OTM strikes?
2. **OI & Price Matrix:** When price rises while call OI falls sharply near a resistance strike, what positioning shift is typically happening—and why must you always verify with price?
3. **Put-Call Ratio (PCR) Context:** Why can an extreme high or low PCR mean different things across index vs. stock chains or across trending vs. range-bound sessions?

## Quick Self-Check

* Do you check **bid-ask spreads and strike liquidity** before placing multi-leg option orders?
* Can you map **breakeven points, max profit, and max loss** for any spread before entering?
