Indian index trades rarely happen in isolation. Checking **intermarket alignment** across **Bank Nifty**, **India VIX**, **Gift Nifty**, and major **global indices** helps you judge whether a move has broad conviction or is running into cross-asset headwinds.

## Cross-Market Relationships to Monitor

* **Nifty 50 & Bank Nifty:** When Bank Nifty leads or confirms Nifty's breakout, institutional participation in financials is supporting the move; when Bank Nifty diverges sharply downward, index rallies often struggle to sustain.
* **Index Price & India VIX:** Steady rallies often coincide with calm or falling India VIX, whereas sharp market sell-offs usually see VIX spike higher. If both spot and VIX rise together—or VIX jumps sharply on a minor dip—hedging demand is rising.
* **Gift Nifty & Global Cues:** Gift Nifty reflects early offshore sentiment before 9:15 AM IST, while US and Asian equity indices shape overnight risk appetite—though domestic price action after the open always has the final say.

## Using Cross-Market Cues Without Overcomplicating

1. **Look for Alignment or Warning Divergence:** Alignment across Nifty, Bank Nifty, and VIX supports higher confidence; sharp divergence warns you to tighten expectations or reduce size.
2. **Let Local Price Action Trigger Execution:** Cross-asset cues provide context and risk awareness, while your chart structure provides exact entry, stop, and target levels.

## Key Takeaways

* **Intermarket context** helps you spot hidden risk (such as rising VIX or lagging Bank Nifty) even when the headline index looks calm.
* Always anchor final trade decisions to **price structure and risk limits**, not external tickers alone.
