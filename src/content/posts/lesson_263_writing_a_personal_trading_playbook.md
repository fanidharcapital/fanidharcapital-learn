## 1. Three Core Sections of a Personal Trading Playbook

A playbook is not a 50-page encyclopedia—it is a **concise operating manual** you can read in ten minutes and refer to cleanly during live trading:

1.  **Core `A+` Setups (`2–3` High-Conviction Playbooks):** For each setup (whether a trend pullback, breakout retest, vertical debit/credit spread, or Iron Condor), specify exact **chart/volatility entry triggers**, **invalidation level**, **target/management rule**, and **typical `DTE` or timeframe**.
2.  **Context & Regime Filters:** State explicitly when a setup is *allowed* vs. *excluded*—such as trend vs. range structure, **`India VIX` / `IV` bands**, liquidity requirements, and avoiding binary event surprises unless specifically planned.
3.  **Non-Negotiable Guardrails:** Lock in your **fixed `1R` max risk per trade**, **portfolio heat cap**, **daily/weekly/monthly circuit breakers**, and **zero-tolerance rules** (such as no unhedged tail expansion, no legging in on fast ticks, and no revenge sizing).

---

## 2. Why Writing It Down Changes Behavior

When a rule lives only in your head, you bend it quietly when a candle looks tempting or a loss stings. Putting your setups, filters, and red lines on paper creates a **clear boundary between planned execution and impulse**—making post-trade grading objective and honest.

---

## 3. Key Takeaways

*   Document **`2–3` core `A+` setups**, explicit **regime/VIX filters**, and **non-negotiable risk guardrails** in a concise written playbook.
*   Written rules remove ambiguity in live sessions and make journal reviews far more actionable.

2.  What kind of **context filters** should sit alongside each setup before you take an entry?
3.  How does a written playbook help you spot **rule drift** during a busy trading week?
