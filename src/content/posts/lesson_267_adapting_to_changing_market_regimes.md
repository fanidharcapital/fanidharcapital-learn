## 1. Rotating Playbooks Across Regimes

Markets do not stay in one personality forever. A breakout trend can shift into wide sideways chop, and low `India VIX` can suddenly expand ahead of macro/earnings catalysts:

*   **Trending Regime:** Favor directional setups (`trend pullbacks`, `breakout retests`, `Bull Call / Bear Put spreads`, or `PMCC/PMCP`) aligned with the dominant swing structure, respecting invalidation if trend momentum stalls.
*   **Range-Bound / High-`IV` Regime:** When price oscillates cleanly between support and resistance and `IV` is elevated, rotate toward **range-bound credit structures** (`Iron Condors`, `Iron Butterflies`, or `S/R credit spreads` with `1R` wing caps and `50%–75%` profit targets).
*   **Volatility Expansion / Catalyst Regime:** When volatility is compressed before major catalysts or sharp breakouts emerge, consider **volatility-buying structures** (`Long Straddles`, `Long Strangles`, or `Backspreads`)—while keeping tight watch on post-event `IV` crush and time decay.

---

## 2. Managing Regime Transitions Safely

*   **Spot Early Shift Clues:** Repeated failed breakouts, messy overlapping candles, `India VIX` regime shifts, or hitting a weekly loss limit often signal that the prior regime is changing.
*   **Trim Size During Transitions:** During uncertain transition phases, **reduce `1R` size** or wait for clean structure confirmation rather than forcing last week's winning playbook onto a new market environment.

---

## 3. Key Takeaways

*   No single strategy wins in every market phase—rotate across **trend, range, and volatility playbooks** as price structure and `VIX`/`IV` shift.
*   **Trim position size** during regime transitions and demand fresh structural confirmation before sizing back up.
