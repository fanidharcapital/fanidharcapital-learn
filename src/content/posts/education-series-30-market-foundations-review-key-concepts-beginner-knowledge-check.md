## 2. Essential Golden Rules Before Placing Any Order

Keep this five-point pre-trade checklist active whenever you interact with your trading terminal:

1. **Never mistake LTP for a guaranteed fill:** Always check the live **Bid-Ask Spread** and **Market Depth** first.
2. **Use Limit Orders by default:** Reserve Market Orders strictly for ultra-liquid stocks when immediate execution matters more than a few ticks of price.
3. **Know your session clock:** Avoid unmonitored market orders during the **9:00–9:15 AM Pre-Open** transition and remember broker intraday square-off cutoffs (**~3:15–3:20 PM**).
4. **Verify corporate action Ex-Dates:** Under **T+1 settlement**, you must buy *before* the **Ex-Date** (on or before the **Cum-Date**) to qualify for dividends, splits, or bonus shares.
5. **Measure net P&L, not gross P&L:** Factor in **STT, Stamp Duty, Exchange/SEBI fees, 18% GST, DP charges, and slippage** to know your true breakeven price.

## 3. Beginner Knowledge Check (10 Questions)

Test your grasp of the **Market Foundations** module before advancing to the next chapter. Try answering each question first, then check the answer key below.

1. **Quote Reading:** A stock shows LTP ₹850.00, Best Bid ₹849.80, and Best Ask ₹850.40. If you place an immediate Market Buy order (assuming sufficient ask quantity at the top level), around what price will your order fill?
2. **Market Depth:** Why is a "thin" order book with large gaps between price levels dangerous for a large Market Order?
3. **Liquidity vs. Volume vs. Volatility:** Which of these three metrics measures *how easily* you can exit a position near the current market price without causing slippage?
4. **Pre-Open Session:** Between what exact minutes does order collection close randomly during the NSE/BSE Pre-Open session?
5. **Closing Price:** How is the official daily Closing Price of a stock calculated on NSE and BSE?
6. **Circuit Breakers:** At what three Nifty 50 / Sensex percentage decline levels do **Market-Wide Index Circuit Breakers** trigger a nationwide trading halt?
7. **Corporate Actions:** You hold 50 shares trading at ₹1,200 each, and the company executes a **1:1 Bonus Issue**. On the Ex-Date, roughly how many shares do you hold and what is the theoretical adjusted share price?
8. **Corporate Action Dates:** Under India's **T+1 settlement cycle**, if a stock's **Ex-Date** for a dividend is Friday, what is the latest trading day you can buy the shares to receive that dividend?
9. **Trading Costs:** Which statutory levy applies **only on the buy side** of an equity trade, and which depository fee applies **only when selling delivery shares**?
10. **Net Profitability:** Why can a trade bought at ₹500.00 and sold at ₹500.40 in Equity Delivery still end up as a net loss?

## 4. Knowledge Check — Answer Key

1. **Around ₹850.40 (the Best Ask):** Immediate buyers must match against the lowest available seller offer (Ask), not the Best Bid or LTP.
2. **Severe Slippage:** A large market order quickly exhausts the small quantities at the top ask levels and fills progressively higher up the order book, worsening your average entry price.
3. **Liquidity:** Liquidity measures ease of execution and spread tightness; Volume measures total shares traded; Volatility measures the speed and magnitude of price swings.
4. **Between 9:07 AM and 9:08 AM:** Random closure during the final minute prevents last-second order manipulation before equilibrium price matching (9:08–9:12 AM).
5. **30-Minute VWAP (3:00 PM – 3:30 PM):** The Volume-Weighted Average Price of trades during the final 30 minutes of the regular session determines the official Closing Price.
6. **10%, 15%, and 20%:** Measured against the previous day's index close, triggering a 45-minute halt (10%), a 1-hour 45-minute halt (15%), or a full-day halt (20%).
7. **100 shares at ~₹600 each:** A 1:1 bonus doubles your share count (50 + 50 = 100) and halves the reference share price, keeping total holding value (~₹60,000) neutral on adjustment.
8. **Thursday (the Cum-Date):** Buying at least one trading day before the Ex-Date ensures T+1 settlement completes on Friday (Record Date).
9. **Stamp Duty (Buy side) and DP Charges (Delivery Sell side):** Stamp duty is levied on purchases, while CDSL/NSDL + broker DP charges apply per scrip per day on delivery sales.
10. **Round-Trip Costs Exceed Gross Gain:** Equity Delivery incurs 0.1% STT on both buy and sell sides, Stamp Duty, Exchange/SEBI fees, 18% GST, and DP charges—which together exceed the ₹0.40 gross gain.
