In **Lesson #130**, we defined uptrends (`HH + HL`) and downtrends (`LH + LL`). In **Lesson #131**, we track the exact moments structure either confirms continuation or warns of an early reversal: **Break of Structure (BOS)** and **Change of Character (CHoCH)**. Mastering this distinction prevents you from shorting a healthy pullback or holding a losing long after an uptrend's backbone has cracked on NSE/BSE charts.

## 1. Break of Structure (BOS) vs. Change of Character (CHoCH)

Both concepts measure price breaking past a confirmed swing pivot—the difference lies in **which direction** the break occurs relative to the active trend:

| Structural Event | Direction Relative to Active Trend | What It Means on the Chart |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bullish BOS** | **With the Uptrend:** Price closes above the most recent confirmed **Swing High** after forming a Higher Low. | **Trend Continuation:** Buyers absorbed pullback supply and established a new Higher High (`HH`). |
| **Bearish BOS** | **With the Downtrend:** Price closes below the most recent confirmed **Swing Low** after forming a Lower High. | **Trend Continuation:** Sellers overwhelmed bounce demand and established a new Lower Low (`LL`). |
| **Bearish CHoCH** | **Against an Uptrend:** Price breaks and closes below the **Higher Low (HL)** that produced the latest Higher High. | **First Structural Crack:** The sequence of Higher Lows is broken. Buyers failed to defend the pullback floor; a range or downtrend may be starting. |
| **Bullish CHoCH** | **Against a Downtrend:** Price breaks and closes above the **Lower High (LH)** that produced the latest Lower Low. | **First Structural Shift:** Sellers failed to defend the rally ceiling; early accumulation or trend reversal is underway. |

---

## 2. The Body-Close Rule: Separating True Breaks From Wick Sweeps

On NSE stocks and Nifty/Bank Nifty charts, intraday liquidity probes frequently poke a few rupees above a Swing High or below a Swing Low and snap right back.

*   **Wick Poke Only ($\neq$ Valid BOS/CHoCH):** If a candle's wick crosses a swing level by `₹3` during the session, yet the candle **closes back inside** the prior range, treat it as a **liquidity sweep** (Lesson #136), not a confirmed structural break.
*   **Decisive Real-Body Close ($=$ Valid BOS/CHoCH):** Require a clean candlestick **body close** beyond the swing pivot on your execution timeframe. A wide-range close with volume expansion confirms genuine institutional commitment.

---

## 3. How to Trade BOS and CHoCH Together

1.  **After a Bullish BOS:** Do not chase the breakout bar at extended highs. Mark the newly validated **Higher Low** below (your structural invalidation floor) and wait for a controlled pullback into support or a broken resistance level.
2.  **After a Bearish CHoCH in an Uptrend:** Immediately stop adding to long positions. A single CHoCH does not guarantee an instant vertical crash—it signals that the easy uptrend phase is over. Wait for either:
    *   A **Lower High (LH)** followed by a **Bearish BOS** to confirm a new downtrend, or
    *   Price reclaiming the broken level if the CHoCH was merely a deep shakeout.

---

## 4. Key Takeaways

*   **BOS** occurs **with the trend** (breaking a Swing High in an uptrend or a Swing Low in a downtrend) and confirms continuation.
*   **CHoCH** occurs **against the trend** (breaking the Higher Low in an uptrend or the Lower High in a downtrend) and marks the first structural warning of a trend shift.
*   Always require a **real-body close** beyond the swing extreme—never rely on a fleeting intraday wick poke alone.
