Following twenty news channels, social media commentary, and conflicting economist forecasts creates analysis paralysis. Professional traders replace constant headline scrolling with a **20-minute weekend macro & flow brief** built around four objective pillars.

## The Four-Pillar Weekly Brief Template

### Pillar 1: Rates, Liquidity & Bond Yields (5 Minutes)
* **Check:** India 10-Year G-Sec yield direction, US 10-Year Treasury yield, and upcoming RBI MPC or Fed calendar dates.
* **Actionable Output:** Classify rate backdrop as *Easing Tailwind*, *Neutral*, or *Tightening Headwind*.

### Pillar 2: Currency & Commodities (5 Minutes)
* **Check:** USD/INR weekly close and Brent crude oil trend.
* **Actionable Output:** Flag whether imported inflation pressure is rising (negative for OMCs/autos/paints) or easing, and note INR impact on IT/pharma.

### Pillar 3: Institutional Flows & Breadth (5 Minutes)
* **Check:** 5-day rolling FII vs. DII cash net flows, FII index futures net long/short ratio, India VIX level, and % of Nifty 500 stocks above 50-DMA.
* **Actionable Output:** Determine whether institutions are accumulating dips, hedging aggressively, or distributing into rallies.

### Pillar 4: Sector Relative Strength Rankings (5 Minutes)
* **Check:** Compare 1-month performance of Nifty Bank, IT, Auto, Pharma, Metal, Capital Goods, FMCG, and Realty against Nifty 50.
* **Actionable Output:** Select the **top 2 leading sectors** for long watchlists and the **bottom 2 lagging sectors** for short/hedge watchlists.

## Rules to Prevent Information Overload

* **Write It Down in Five Bullet Points:** If your weekly macro view takes more than half a page, it is too noisy to execute.
* **Separate Scheduled Catalysts From Noise:** Mark exact dates and times of CPI prints, RBI decisions, and monthly expiry on your desk calendar so you never get surprised intraday.
