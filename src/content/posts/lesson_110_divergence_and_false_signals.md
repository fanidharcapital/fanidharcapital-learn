## 1. Regular Divergence vs. Hidden Divergence

Not all divergences warn of reversals. Depending on whether you compare **trend extremes** or **pullback swings**, divergence signals either **potential exhaustion (Regular)** or **trend continuation (Hidden)**:

| Divergence Type | Price Swing Pattern | Indicator Swing Pattern (`RSI / MACD / OBV`) | Market Meaning | Tactical Bias |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Regular Bullish Divergence** | **Lower Low (LL)** | **Higher Low (HL)** | Downside momentum is decelerating even as price dips slightly below its prior trough | **Potential Reversal Upward:** Watch for support reclaim or CHoCH before buying |
| **Regular Bearish Divergence** | **Higher High (HH)** | **Lower High (LH)** | Upside thrust is weakening despite price nudging to a marginal new peak | **Potential Reversal Downward / Profit-Booking:** Tighten trailing stops; wait for breakdown before shorting |
| **Hidden Bullish Divergence** | **Higher Low (HL)** | **Lower Low (LL)** | Oscillator resets deeply during a shallow, healthy price pullback inside an active uptrend | **Bullish Continuation:** Confirms strong underlying price structure after momentum reset |
| **Hidden Bearish Divergence** | **Lower High (LH)** | **Higher High (HH)** | Oscillator spikes sharply on a weak counter-trend bounce without reclaiming prior price swing high | **Bearish Continuation:** Signals relief bounce is losing steam below overhead resistance |

## 2. Why Divergences Produce False Signals in Strong Trends

Retail traders frequently treat Regular Bearish Divergence as an automatic short trigger—and get squeezed repeatedly during institutional bull runs on NSE/BSE:

1. **Multi-Swing Divergence Stacking:** In a powerful uptrend (`ADX > 25`), a sector leader can print **two, three, or even four consecutive bearish divergences** on the Daily RSI or MACD Histogram while price keeps stair-stepping higher. Momentum merely moderates from "explosive" to "steady" without reversing trend direction.
2. **Timeframe Mismatch:** A bearish divergence on a 15-minute or 1-hour chart often resolves through a **2-day sideways consolidation** rather than a major downtrend when the Daily and Weekly structures remain firmly bullish.
3. **Indicator Over-Sensitivity:** Fast settings (such as a 5-period RSI or fast Stochastic) generate frequent micro-divergences on routine intrabar noise. Stick to standard settings (`14-RSI`, `12-26-9 MACD`) on **Daily or Weekly** swings for meaningful structural warnings.

## 3. The Three-Step Divergence Execution Filter

Never enter a trade on divergence alone. Require **price action to confirm** that the divergence has actually shifted order flow:

1. **Location Check (Key Structural Zone):** Does the divergence occur right at a major **Weekly/Daily Support or Resistance band**, a psychological round number, or an **Exhaustion Gap** (Lesson #100)? Divergences in the middle of nowhere carry low odds.
2. **Momentum Hook:** Wait for the oscillator to turn decisively—such as RSI hooking back above `30–40` (bullish) or MACD crossing its Signal Line.
3. **Mandatory Price Confirmation (CHoCH / Trendline Break):** Require price itself to break a **local swing high/low** (Change of Character from Lesson #95) or close across a short-term pullback trendline before risking capital.
