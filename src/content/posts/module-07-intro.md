Welcome to **Module 07: Options Market**. This introductory page gives you a clear summary of what **Days 181–210** cover across options fundamentals and option-chain analysis.

---

## What You Will Master in Module 07

* **Calls, Puts & Payoff Basics (`Days 181–188`):** Buying and selling calls and puts, obligations vs. rights, break-even points, payoff diagrams at expiry, and asymmetric risk/reward profiles.
* **Moneyness, Pricing & Time Decay (`Days 189–196`):** `ITM`, `ATM`, and `OTM` strikes, intrinsic value vs. extrinsic/time value, time decay acceleration near expiry, and how strike choice alters probability.
* **Implied Volatility & Option Chain (`Days 197–203`):** Historical vs. implied volatility (`IV`), `IV` rank/percentile concepts, reading option chain strikes, open interest (`OI`), and `PCR` context.
* **Options Traps & Module Review (`Days 204–210`):** Why buyers lose to decay or `IV` crush, why sellers face tail risk, liquidity/bid-ask checks, case studies, and the Module 07 Capstone checklist.
