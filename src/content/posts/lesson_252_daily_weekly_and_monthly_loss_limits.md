In **Lesson #251**, we capped total open portfolio heat. In **Lesson #252**, we install **time-based account circuit breakers**—**Daily, Weekly, and Monthly Loss Limits** that automatically halt trading when a choppy session, tilt episode, or hostile market regime strikes.

---

## 1. Tiered Account Circuit Breakers

Just as stock exchanges trigger market-wide circuit breakers during extreme volatility, professional traders enforce personal loss tiers:

| Time Horizon | Example Hard Limit (`R`-Multiples or `%`) | Mandatory Action When Triggered |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Daily Loss Limit** | **`-2R` to `-3R`** (e.g., `2%–3%` of capital) | **Close all intraday trades / halt new entries** for the rest of the day; step away from screens. |
| **Weekly Loss Limit** | **`-4R` to `-5R`** (e.g., `4%–5%` of capital) | **Stop trading for the remainder of the week**; audit journal for regime mismatch or rule violations. |
| **Monthly Loss Limit** | **`-6R` to `-8R`** (e.g., `6%–8%` of capital) | **Mandatory multi-day reset + cut `1R` size by `50%`** until consistency returns. |

---

## 2. Why Hard Limits Save Both Capital and Confidence

*   **Stops "Spiral Days":** Without a daily stop, one frustrating morning loss easily snowballs into five revenge trades before `3:30 PM`. A hard daily cap guarantees no single bad day can wreck your month.
*   **Detects Regime Shifts Early:** Hitting a weekly or monthly limit often signals that market conditions (e.g., sudden low-volume chop or spiked volatility) no longer fit your current playbook—giving you a structured pause to adapt rather than bleed out.

---

## 3. Key Takeaways

*   Pre-commit to **Daily, Weekly, and Monthly loss limits** before the week begins—never negotiate them mid-session.
*   Hitting a circuit breaker triggers an **immediate pause and objective review**, protecting both capital and mindset.
