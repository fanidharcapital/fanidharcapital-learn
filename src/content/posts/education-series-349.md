![Case Study — Eliminating Mid-Trade Discretionary Drift With Codified Rules](/images/lessons/lesson-349.png)

Even traders who know technical analysis and options inside out can lose money when rules are left unwritten and open to improvisation during live market hours.

## Before vs. After Codifying Rules

* **Improvised Session:** A trader spots a green candle mid-morning without checking higher-timeframe structure, enters late without a pre-calculated stop, moves the stop wider when price dips, and exits early on a small bounce out of relief.
* **Codified Session:** Before market open, the trader writes exact eligibility rules, entry trigger, structural stop-loss, position size, and target/trail rules. When price reaches the exact invalidation level, the exit happens cleanly without debate or hesitation.

## Key Execution Lessons

* **Write Rules Before the Market Opens:** Decisions made under live price pressure are far more prone to bias than pre-planned rules.
* **Audit Rule Adherence Separately From P&L:** A clean loss that followed every rule is a *process win*; a lucky gain made by breaking risk rules is still a *process hazard*.
