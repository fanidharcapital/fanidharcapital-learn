![Order Book Depth, Tape Reading & Absorption vs. Exhaustion](/images/lessons/lesson-304.png)

Two candles can touch the exact same resistance line for completely opposite reasons: large supply may be **absorbing** incoming buyers, or aggressive sellers may be **exhausted** right before a squeeze. Understanding **absorption vs. exhaustion** helps you anticipate whether a key level will hold or give way.

## Absorption vs. Exhaustion at Key Levels

* **Institutional Absorption:** Heavy volume hits a support or resistance zone, yet price refuses to break through—showing a large buyer or seller quietly filling big size without chasing price. Once the resting size is absorbed, price often breaks cleanly in the direction of the absorbing player.
* **Trend Exhaustion:** After a long directional leg, price reaches a major level on a climactic spike or wide-range candle, stalls immediately, and prints overlapping candles or long wicks as late momentum traders run out of fuel.

## Practical Clues Without Over-Reliance on Level-2 Quotes

1. **Price Progress vs. Volume:** High volume with *minimal* net candle progress at support/resistance points to absorption; high volume with *stalling* follow-through after a big run signals exhaustion.
2. **Level-2 Depth Caution:** Displayed bid/ask walls in liquid index and stock books can be cancelled or refreshed in milliseconds; treat order-book depth as secondary context rather than a standalone signal.
3. **Confirm With Structure:** Always wait for a candle close or structural trigger (BOS/CHoCH) rather than guessing the exact tick of a reversal.

## Key Takeaways

* **Absorption** builds pressure for a potential breakout or reversal once resting liquidity is filled; **exhaustion** reflects spent momentum after an extended move.
* Never treat a single visible quote wall in market depth as a guaranteed floor or ceiling.
