## 1. The Four Types of Price Gaps

A **gap** occurs when a stock or index opens significantly above the prior session's high (gap-up) or below the prior session's low (gap-down), leaving an empty price zone with zero traded transactions in between. Not every gap behaves the same—context and volume define which of the four gap types you are looking at:

| Gap Type | Trend Location | Volume Signature | Fill Behavior | Strategic Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Common Gap (Area Gap)** | Inside a flat sideways range or congested base | Low to average volume | Typically fills quickly (within days or a couple of weeks) | Routine overnight order imbalance; rarely marks a new trend |
| **Breakaway Gap** | Breaking cleanly out of a multi-week base, triangle, or key S/R zone | **Heavy surge (2x–4x 20-DMA volume)** | Often stays **unfilled** (or only tests the upper rim of the gap as support) | **Trend Ignition:** Institutional demand overwhelms supply after earnings or a major catalyst |
| **Runaway / Measuring Gap** | Roughly halfway through an already established, strong trend | Moderate to strong volume | Usually stays **unfilled** during the active trend leg | Signals strong momentum; distance from trend start to gap often projects a similar remaining leg |
| **Exhaustion Gap** | Near the end of a steep, extended multi-week rally or decline | Extreme climax volume followed by weak follow-through | **Fills rapidly** as price reverses back through the gap zone | Final wave of FOMO buyers or panic sellers; warns of an impending reversal |

### Practical Rule on "Do All Gaps Get Filled?"
* **Myth:** "Every gap must fill immediately."
* **Reality:** **Common** and **Exhaustion** gaps fill quickly, whereas **Breakaway** and **Runaway** gaps on strong NSE leaders frequently act as **institutional support zones** for months before ever being revisited. Never short a high-volume Breakaway Gap blindly just expecting a gap-fill.

## 2. Indian Equities Market Sessions (NSE / BSE Timing Dynamics)

Because Indian equities trade continuously from **9:15 AM to 3:30 PM IST** (with a 15-minute pre-open auction), overnight global cues (US markets, GIFT Nifty, crude oil, RBI/SEBI announcements, and post-market corporate earnings) concentrate into specific intraday windows:

1. **Pre-Open Session (9:00 AM – 9:15 AM IST):**
   * Order collection runs from 9:00–9:08 AM, followed by order matching and equilibrium price discovery.
   * Large overnight news or quarterly results settle here—creating the initial gap at 9:15 AM.
2. **Opening Volatility Window (9:15 AM – 9:45 AM IST):**
   * Highest retail participation, overnight stop-loss triggers, and rapid price discovery.
   * **Discipline Filter:** Many false breakouts and gap-and-crap reversals occur in the first 15–30 minutes. Letting the **Opening Range** establish helps separate genuine institutional follow-through from emotional opening spikes.
3. **Mid-Day Consolidation Window (11:30 AM – 2:00 PM IST):**
   * Liquidity and volume typically cool off into narrower ranges; breakouts during this window have a higher failure rate unless driven by breaking news.
4. **Institutional Closing Window (2:30 PM – 3:30 PM IST):**
   * European market overlap, FII/DII execution desks, mutual fund flows, and intraday square-offs drive the **true structural close** (calculated via the 3:00–3:30 PM weighted average price on NSE).
   * A gap-up stock that holds near its intraday high through 3:15 PM confirms genuine institutional accumulation.

## 4. Key Takeaways

* **Breakaway Gaps** on heavy volume out of a clean base signal institutional ignition; **Exhaustion Gaps** after an extended run signal climax risk.
* On NSE/BSE, **9:15–9:45 AM** reflects emotional order clearing, while **2:30–3:30 PM** reflects institutional conviction and sets the true daily close.
* Always wait for price action to confirm whether an opening gap **holds above the opening range** or **fails back inside the prior day's range**.
