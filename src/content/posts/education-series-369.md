## Overview

Headline returns are an illusion until adjusted for two silent eroders of purchasing power: **taxation** and **inflation**. An investment yielding 7.5% nominally can deliver negative real wealth growth once slab-rate tax and 5.5%–6% inflation are deducted.

---

## The Post-Tax Real Return Formula

To evaluate any asset class accurately, convert nominal yield into post-tax real return:

$$\text{Post-Tax Return} = R_{\text{nominal}} \times (1 - t)$$

$$\text{Real Return} = \frac{1 + \text{Post-Tax Return}}{1 + \pi} - 1 \approx \text{Post-Tax Return} - \pi$$

Where $R_{\text{nominal}}$ is the pre-tax yield, $t$ is the applicable marginal tax rate, and $\pi$ is the inflation rate.

## Hidden Drags on Active Traders

1. **Annual Accrual vs. Deferred Tax:** Fixed deposit interest is taxed every financial year even if reinvested, interrupting compounding. Equity capital gains are taxed only when realized.
2. **Turnover Friction in F&O:** Securities Transaction Tax (STT), exchange transaction charges, SEBI fees, stamp duty, and GST apply on every trade regardless of profitability—requiring high gross edge to stay net-positive.
3. **Lifestyle Inflation:** Personal education, healthcare, and urban living costs often inflate faster than headline CPI, demanding growth assets for long-term goals.

---

## Key Takeaways

* **Measure wealth in purchasing power:** A positive nominal return that trails post-tax inflation represents a guaranteed loss in real terms.
* **Optimize tax timing:** Deferring realization on compounding assets preserves capital inside the portfolio longer.
* **Track net-of-cost P&L:** In active trading, evaluate strategy expectancy after all statutory levies and taxes.
