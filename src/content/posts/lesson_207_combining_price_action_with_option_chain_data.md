## 1. The Three-Lens Options Decision Stack

Just like in futures (Lesson #174), keep **price structure** at the top so option-chain data confirms or refines your view rather than inventing random trades:

1.  **Lens 1 — Chart Structure & Levels (Primary Bias & Invalidation):**
    *   Identify trend direction, key support/resistance zones, breakout/retest structure, and where your trade is structurally wrong.
2.  **Lens 2 — Option Chain (`OI`, `ΔOI` & `PCR`):**
    *   Look for **high Call OI / Call Writing** near resistance zones and **high Put OI / Put Writing** near support zones.
    *   Notice whether price is **respecting** an OI wall (bouncing/rejecting with structure) or **breaking through** while writers unwind (`falling OI` / covering).
3.  **Lens 3 — Volatility & Time Context (`IV`, `India VIX` & `DTE`):**
    *   Check whether premiums are cheap or inflated (`IV` / `VIX`), whether a major event (`earnings` / `policy`) is imminent (`IV crush` risk), and how much time remains until expiry (`Theta` pace).

---

## 2. How to Avoid Common Option-Chain Traps

*   **Don't Fade a Clean Breakout Just Because of Call OI:** OI walls can act as magnets or reaction zones, **not brick walls**. If price breaks cleanly above a high-Call-OI strike with strong momentum and Call writers unwind, fighting the trend because *"there's big Call OI there"* can be costly.
*   **Don't Trade Option Chain Without a Stop-Loss:** Option-chain clues are probabilistic context—your invalidation level and `1R` position size still govern every trade.

---

## 3. Key Takeaways

*   Combine **Chart Structure (Lens 1)**, **Option Chain `OI`/`ΔOI`/`PCR` (Lens 2)**, and **Volatility/`IV`/`DTE` Context (Lens 3)**.
*   Treat **OI walls** as dynamic reaction/inflection zones—never as unbreakable guarantees against a real breakout.
