Welcome to **Module 04: Technical Analysis**. This introductory page gives you a clear summary of what **Days 91–120** cover across core technical indicators and chart frameworks.

---

## What You Will Master in Module 04

* **Trends, Levels & Classical Structures (`Days 91–98`):** Multi-timeframe trend analysis, horizontal zones, pivot points, ranges, breakouts, pullbacks, and classic chart formations.
* **Moving Averages & Trend Indicators (`Days 99–106`):** `SMA`, `EMA`, dynamic support/resistance, moving average crossovers/slopes, `ADX`, and trend-following vs. sideways behavior.
* **Momentum Oscillators & Volume Tools (`Days 107–113`):** `RSI`, `MACD`, divergence vs. continuation, `VWAP`, volume spikes/dry-ups, and volatility bands (`ATR`, `Bollinger Bands`).
* **Indicator Discipline & Module Review (`Days 114–120`):** Avoiding indicator overload, combining price with one or two non-redundant tools, false signal filtering, case studies, and the Module 04 Capstone checklist.
