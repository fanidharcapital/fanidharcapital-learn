In **Lesson #145**, we timed the entry using candle close confirmation. In **Lesson #146**, we complete the trade management trifecta by defining **where to exit**. This lesson is critical: your stop-loss must not be based on how much money you are willing to lose (e.g., *"I'll risk ₹5,000 on this trade"*), and your target must not be based on a random percentage gain (e.g., *"I always take 10% profit"*). **Exits must be anchored to market structure.**

## 1. Structural Stop-Loss Placement (Anchoring to Invalidation)

Your stop-loss is your "get out of jail free" card. It must be placed at a point where your original trading thesis is **proven wrong**.

### For Long Trades (Buying Support / Breakout):
The thesis is: *"Demand is taking control at this level, and price will now make a higher high."*
*   **Validation:** Price holds the level.
*   **Invalidation:** Price makes a new low.
*   **Stop-Loss Placement (The Buffer):** Place your stop-loss **cleanly below the extreme wick tip** of the rejection candle (Hammer, Buying Tail) or the swing low that you are trading against. Always add a small buffer (e.g., `₹0.50` for stocks under `₹100`, `₹2–₹5` for stocks over `₹1,000`, or `5-10 points` on Nifty/Bank Nifty) to account for routine liquidity sweeps (Lesson #136).

### For Short Trades (Selling Resistance / Breakdown):
The thesis is: *"Supply is overwhelming demand at this level, and price will now make a lower low."*
*   **Validation:** Price holds the level.
*   **Invalidation:** Price makes a new high.
*   **Stop-Loss Placement (The Buffer):** Place your stop-loss **cleanly above the extreme wick tip** of the rejection candle (Shooting Star, Selling Tail) or the swing high you are fading, plus a small buffer.

## 2. Profit Target Placement (Anchoring to the Next Structure)

Your profit target must be based on where the opposing force (buyers for shorts, sellers for longs) is naturally waiting. **Targets are anchored to the next higher timeframe Support/Resistance zone.**

### For Long Trades:
*   **Immediate Target:** The midpoint of the nearest higher-timeframe **Supply/Resistance zone** (Lesson #132).
*   **Conservative Target:** The very beginning (outer edge) of the nearest Supply Zone.
*   **Aggressive Target:** The midpoint or extreme of the Supply Zone (only if the trend is exceptionally strong).

### For Down Trades:
*   **Immediate Target:** The midpoint of the nearest higher-timeframe **Demand/Support zone**.
*   **Conservative Target:** The very beginning (outer edge) of the nearest Demand Zone.

### Managing the Trade (Scaling Out):
Price rarely moves in a straight line. If your target is `₹200` away, scale out as structure points get hit.
1.  **Sell 50% at First Target:** Secure profit at the first major structural level (e.g., a 1:2 Reward/Risk ratio).
2.  **Trail 50% to Breakeven:** Move your stop to entry.
3.  **Hold for Further Targets:** Let the remaining 50% run toward the next higher target, trailing your stop below newly formed Higher Lows (on H1/D1 charts).

## 3. Calculating Reward-to-Risk (R:R)

Before entering, you must know your R:R. It must be at least **1:2 or better** for a high-probability setup.

*   **Formula:** `(Target Price - Entry Price) / (Entry Price - Stop-Loss Price) > 2`
*   **Example (Long Trade):**
    *   **Entry:** ₹505
    *   **Stop-Loss:** ₹497 (8-point risk)
    *   **First Target:** ₹529 (24-point reward)
    *   **Reward/Risk:** 24 / 8 = **1:3 (Excellent Setup)**

If your stop-loss must be `₹8` wide based on structure, but the nearest target is only `₹8` away, your R:R is 1:1. **Do not take the trade.** The reward does not justify the risk of structural invalidation.

---

## 4. Key Takeaways

*   **Stop-Loss Placement:** Anchor to **structural invalidation points** (just beyond the rejection wick or swing pivot you traded against), plus a buffer.
*   **Profit Target Placement:** Anchor to the **next major higher-timeframe Support/Resistance zone**. Do not use arbitrary rupee/percentage targets.
*   **Reward-to-Risk:** Only execute trades where the distance to the structural target is at least **twice** the distance to the structural stop-loss (`1:2 R:R` minimum).
