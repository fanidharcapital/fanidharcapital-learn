Across **12 weeks and 365 lessons**, the curriculum builds from ground-floor market mechanics all the way to multi-regime options structure, quantitative risk controls, and practical case studies. Seeing how every layer connects turns isolated concepts into one coherent framework.

## How the 12-Week Arc Fits Together

| Curriculum Layer | Core Focus | How It Supports Better Decisions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Foundations, Clearing & Regulation** | Exchanges, depositories, T+1, surveillance, compliance | Keeps account mechanics, settlement, and legal/regulatory boundaries clear |
| **Fundamentals & Valuation** | Business models, moats, statements, cash flows, multiples | Grounds directional and investment conviction in underlying economics |
| **Price Action, Structure & Liquidity** | Trends, S/R, VWAP, Value Areas, sweeps, BOS/CHoCH, traps | Defines clean entry/exit zones and realistic structural invalidation |
| **Futures, Options & Volatility** | Basis, rollovers, payoffs, OI/PCR, Greeks, HV/IV, skew, term structure | Matches instrument and structure to directional, range, or volatility regimes |
| **Risk Math, Playbooks & Case Studies** | Expectancy, drawdowns, ATR sizing, journaling, SOPs, real scenarios | Protects capital through drawdowns and turns knowledge into repeatable habit |

## Key Takeaways

* No single chart pattern, indicator, or option structure works in every market environment—**context, risk sizing, and discipline** tie the whole system together.
* Revisiting earlier foundational modules alongside advanced case studies sharpens both speed and judgment.
