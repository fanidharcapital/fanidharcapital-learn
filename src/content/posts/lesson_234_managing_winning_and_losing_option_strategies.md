## 1. Taking Profits Early vs. Holding to Expiry

*   **The `50%–75%` Credit Profit Rule:** On credit spreads, Iron Condors, and Iron Butterflies, once you have captured **`50% to 75%` of the maximum credit**, closing early locks in the bulk of your reward while eliminating late-expiry **Gamma spikes** and gap risk for the remaining pennies.
*   **Scaling Out on Debit Spreads:** On directional debit spreads (`Bull Call` / `Bear Put`), consider booking partial or full profits near your technical target zone or when the spread reaches `70%–85%` of maximum width, rather than risking a late reversal just to squeeze out the final few points.

---

## 2. Pre-Defined Stop-Losses & Avoiding Over-Tinkering

*   **Hard Exit Triggers Before Entry:** Define your exit **before** placing the order—either via **spread price** (e.g., closing a credit spread if its buyback cost reaches `1.5x–2.0x` the initial credit received, or capping loss at `1R`) or via **underlying invalidation** (e.g., a decisive close beyond the support/resistance level that justified the trade).
*   **Why Over-Tinkering Erodes Your Edge:** Constantly adding legs, shifting strikes on minor intraday noise, or morphing a clean defined-risk trade into a complex multi-lot mess racks up **bid-ask slippage, brokerage, and STT/exchange charges**—often turning a small planned loss into a much larger unforced error.

---

## 3. Key Takeaways

*   Locking in **`50%–75%` of max credit** early protects gains from late-expiry Gamma surges and frees up margin for cleaner setups.
*   Respect pre-defined **stop-loss / invalidation rules** and resist impulsive **over-tinkering**.
