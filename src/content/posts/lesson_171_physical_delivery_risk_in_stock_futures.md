In **Lesson #170**, we mapped the four core futures risks. In **Lesson #171**, we focus on an operational hazard specific to Indian single-stock derivatives: **Physical Delivery Risk in Stock Futures**. Unlike index futures (which are cash-settled), all stock futures on NSE are **physically settled** at expiry—meaning holding a contract past expiry cutoffs creates real share-delivery and full-value funding obligations.

## 1. What Physical Settlement Requires at Expiry

If a single-stock futures position remains open through expiry settlement:

*   **Long Stock Future (`Buy`):** You must take delivery of the full lot of underlying equity shares and pay **100% of the contract settlement value** (not just initial margin), plus applicable statutory/delivery charges.
*   **Short Stock Future (`Sell`):** You must deliver the exact lot quantity of underlying shares into clearing settlement. Failure to deliver results in **short-delivery auction penalties** and close-out charges.

---

## 2. Expiry-Week Margin Escalation & Broker Cutoffs

Because physical delivery involves full contract value rather than leveraged margin, clearing corporations and brokers progressively tighten risk controls during **expiry week**:

1.  **Staggered Delivery Margin Hikes:** In the final days before expiry, additional **delivery margins** (often scaling sharply over the last few sessions) are levied on open stock futures positions.
2.  **Broker Square-Off Windows:** If your account lacks the full funds (for long positions) or demat shares/delivery margin (for short positions) before your broker's expiry cutoff time, the broker's risk desk will **square off your position**—often near the close when spreads are wide.
3.  **Interaction With F&O Ban:** If a stock enters **F&O ban** during expiry week (covered next in **Lesson #172**), traders can only close existing positions—not initiate fresh positions—limiting rollover flexibility.

---

## 3. Simple Rule to Avoid Unwanted Delivery

*   If your goal is directional or swing price capture—not taking or giving physical share delivery—**exit or roll over stock futures well before expiry-week delivery margin hikes and broker square-off deadlines**.

---

## 4. Key Takeaways

*   **Stock futures on NSE are physically settled**, requiring full contract payment (for longs) or share delivery (for shorts) at expiry.
*   **Delivery margins escalate** during expiry week, and brokers enforce strict pre-expiry square-off cutoffs if delivery obligations aren't funded.
*   Close or roll stock futures **before expiry-week delivery windows** unless you explicitly intend to settle physically.
