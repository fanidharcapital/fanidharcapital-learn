## 1. Buying Tails vs. Selling Wicks

Shadows are not passive lines; they represent active price rejection where liquidity shifted abruptly:

| Footprint | Visual Signature | Order-Flow Story |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Buying Tail (Long Lower Wick)** | Long shadow below the real body, with the close near the upper third of the candle | Sellers pushed price down aggressively intraday, hit institutional demand, and buyers absorbed all supply to drive price back up before the close. |
| **Selling Wick (Long Upper Wick)** | Long shadow above the real body, with the close near the lower third of the candle | Buyers bid price higher intraday, ran into heavy overhead supply/profit-booking, and sellers forced price back down before the close. |

*   **Rule of Thumb:** A wick that makes up **at least 50% to 65%** of the total candle range signals decisive rejection of that extreme.

---

## 2. The Marubozu: Uncontested Session Control

*Marubozu* (Japanese for "cropped" or "shaved") describes a wide-range candlestick with virtually **no upper or lower shadows**:

*   **Bullish Marubozu ($\text{Open} \approx \text{Low}$, $\text{Close} \approx \text{High}$):** Buyers took control at the very first tick and pressed prices higher into the closing bell without ever letting sellers push price below the open.
*   **Bearish Marubozu ($\text{Open} \approx \text{High}$, $\text{Close} \approx \text{Low}$):** Sellers dominated from the open and liquidated positions steadily into the close without any meaningful bounce.

**Why It Matters on NSE/BSE:** A Marubozu that breaks out of a multi-day range or opens right at the day's low (`Open = Low` scanner setups at 9:15 AM) reflects institutional urgency rather than retail noise.

---

## 3. The Session Control Spectrum

Rather than memorizing dozens of candle names, grade every completed candle along a **4-stage Session Control Spectrum**:

1.  **Total Bullish Control (Bullish Marubozu / Wide Green Body):** Open near Low, Close near High. Maximum upward momentum.
2.  **Bullish Recovery (Long Buying Tail):** Sellers led early, buyers won the second half and closed strong. Signals demand stepping in at support.
3.  **Bearish Rejection (Long Selling Wick):** Buyers led early, sellers won the second half and closed weak. Signals supply capping resistance.
4.  **Total Bearish Control (Bearish Marubozu / Wide Red Body):** Open near High, Close near Low. Maximum downward momentum.

---

## 4. Practical Application on NSE / BSE

1.  **Watch How Price Reacts at Key Zones:** When Nifty or an NSE stock dips into a prior swing low and prints a sharp **Buying Tail** on a 15-minute or Daily chart, sellers have exhausted their immediate inventory.
2.  **Beware of Chasing Long Selling Wicks:** If a stock breaks above resistance intraday at 11:00 AM, only to fade by 3:30 PM and leave a tall **Selling Wick** closing back below the breakout level, treat it as a failed breakout trap—not strength.
3.  **Check Follow-Through on the Next Candle:** True directional pressure holds its ground. After a Bullish Marubozu, pullbacks on the next 1–2 candles should stay shallow (ideally holding above the midpoint of the Marubozu body).

---

## 5. Key Takeaways

*   **Buying tails** show demand absorbing supply below; **selling wicks** show supply rejecting higher prices above.
*   A **Marubozu** represents one-sided session control from open to close with minimal shadow rejection.
*   Grading candles by **who controlled the close** eliminates guesswork and keeps your focus on live order flow.
