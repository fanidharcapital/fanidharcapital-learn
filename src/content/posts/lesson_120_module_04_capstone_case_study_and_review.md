## 1. End-to-End Case Study Blueprint (Illustrative NSE Swing Setup)

Suppose you are evaluating a liquid NSE equity for a **multi-day to multi-week swing trade** using a **₹5,00,000** dedicated trading allocation and a **1% fixed risk rule** (**₹5,000 maximum loss per trade**):

| Step | Module 04 Discipline | What You Check on the Chart & Plan | Concrete Execution Output |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Regime & Relative Strength** | Sector Rotation & RS (#118) | Is the stock's sector index outperforming or holding firm vs. Nifty 50, with clean market breadth? | Confirm sector is in a constructive regime rather than a broad breakdown |
| **2. Top-Down Cascade** | Multi-Timeframe Analysis (#111) | **Weekly:** Primary uptrend intact below/above major zone. **Daily:** Price pulls back into a prior breakout resistance-turned-support shelf (**polarity flip**, #96) near a rising 20/50-EMA (#101–#102). | Higher-timeframe bias and setup zone aligned |
| **3. Non-Redundant Confluence** | Indicators & Volume (#103–#110) | **Structure:** Higher Low (`HL`) holds. **Momentum:** 14-day RSI cools into `42–50` and hooks up without bearish divergence. **Volume:** Pullback volume contracts; bounce candle shows expanding volume/delivery interest (#107). | Clean 4-pillar alignment without indicator clutter (#109) |
| **4. Entry Trigger & Order Type** | Entry Triggers & Slippage (#112) | Wait for a confirmed **bullish trigger candle close** inside the support/EMA zone (or breakout above a minor pullback trendline)—avoiding blind mid-drop buys or wide-spread opening spikes. | Planned entry at **₹500** via a controlled **Limit / Stop-Limit** order |
| **5. Stop-Loss & Position Size** | Invalidation & Sizing (#106, #113–#114) | Place invalidation stop below the pullback swing low with a **14-day ATR buffer** (`₹20` stop distance = `1R` at **₹480**). Compute quantity: $\frac{₹5,000}{₹20} = \mathbf{250\text{ shares}}$ (`₹1,25,000` notional). | Hard stop at **₹480**; exact size **250 shares** (`1R = ₹5,000`) |
| **6. Target, Expectancy & Exit** | R:R, Expectancy & Stops (#113, #115) | Identify logical overhead supply/resistance at **₹550** (`₹50` upside = **`2.5R`**, or **`1:2.5 R:R`**). Decide beforehand whether to take partial profits or trail stops along higher lows. | Minimum `1:2+` structural R:R before risking capital |
| **7. Post-Trade Review & Journal** | Psychology & Journaling (#116–#117) | Log entry, stop, exit, realized `R` multiple (e.g., `+2.2R` or `-1.0R`), chart screenshots, and emotional state (no FOMO, revenge trading, or overtrading). | Empirical data added to sample—not judged on one trade |

## 2. Module 04 Master Synthesis (Lessons #91–#120)

* **Price Structure & Supply/Demand Lead:** Candlesticks, `HH/HL` vs. `LH/LL` swings, horizontal support/resistance, polarity flips, chart patterns, and volume/gaps establish **where** key decisions happen and **who** has control.
* **Indicators Serve Structure, Never Replace It:** Moving averages, ribbons, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, ATR, VWAP/OBV, Stochastic, and ADX help filter trend regime, gauge momentum, measure volatility, and spot divergences—provided you avoid indicator clutter and wait for price confirmation.
* **Risk & Process Decide Longevity:** Top-down timeframe alignment, disciplined entry triggers, true technical invalidation stops, rupee-based position sizing, positive expectancy (`R:R` + win rate), emotional self-control, and honest journaling turn chart reading into a repeatable craft.
