Welcome to **Module 03: Fundamental Analysis**. This introductory page gives you a clear summary of what **Days 61–90** cover as you learn to evaluate underlying businesses.

---

## What You Will Master in Module 03

* **Business Models & Economic Moats (`Days 61–68`):** Revenue drivers, unit economics, pricing power, switching costs, network effects, industry structure, and competitive durability.
* **Financial Statements & Cash Flows (`Days 69–76`):** Reading the Profit & Loss statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement together, working capital dynamics, and earnings vs. cash conversion.
* **Key Financial Ratios & Valuation (`Days 77–83`):** `ROE`, `ROCE`, debt-to-equity, interest coverage, margins, `P/E`, `P/B`, `EV/EBITDA`, `PEG`, and growth vs. valuation context.
* **Governance, Red Flags & Synthesis (`Days 84–90`):** Promoter holding/pledging, related-party transactions, accounting quality checks, sector-specific nuances, case studies, and the Module 03 Capstone checklist.
